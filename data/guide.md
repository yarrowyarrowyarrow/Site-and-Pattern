# How to plan your yard

## Your first yard, in five steps

1. **Find your yard.** On the **Site** tab, type your address and press **Find**, or press **Click my yard on the map** and click it.
2. **Outline a bed.** Press **▱ Shape** above the map, choose *Garden Bed*, press **Draw on the map**, click the bed's corners and click the first corner again to close it.
3. **Plan my yard.** Press **Plan my yard** in the strip above the map. It fills the bed with plants native to your area, and **Try another** gives a different plan.
4. **Look at the overview.** **Design → Overview** says what the plan feeds, when it flowers, how full it is and what it will take.
5. **Print the plan.** **Share → Export → Export PDF…** gives the overview, the ground preparation, a shopping list, a planting map and a care calendar.

## If something goes wrong

- **Edit → Undo** (Ctrl+Z) takes back the last change. A whole plan comes back off in one step.
- **File → Save** keeps your design. Next time, the start screen lists it under *Your designs*.
- The rest of the tools wait behind **View → Advanced tools**. Turning them on or off changes nothing in your design.

## Learning about native plants

**Help → Start Screen… → Learn about native plants** opens a wild landscape you can walk through and plant in, a quiz, and short lessons.

## It is not an identification guide

The photographs and descriptions help you choose what to plant. Check a plant's identity with a book or a person before you collect it from the wild or eat it.

## What leaves your computer

The address you type goes to OpenStreetMap's address search when you press Find. Your yard's location goes to Open-Meteo and SoilGrids for its climate and soil, and the map's tile servers see the part of the map on screen. When you ask, a box around your yard goes to Overpass, Natural Resources Canada or the City of Edmonton. Nothing else leaves: no account, no analytics. **Help → Where This Data Came From** credits every service.

## Feedback

**Help → Send Feedback…** copies your message, ready to email, with no account needed.
