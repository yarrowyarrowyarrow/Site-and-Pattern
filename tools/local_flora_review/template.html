    Getting your rulings into the app

    Your rulings are kept in this page's own database. They reach the app and the website when a Claude Code session folds them in: it reads them from here, checks each one (a ruling needs a reason, and none can make a plant native here that VASCAN does not record native in Alberta), writes them to data/local_flora_rulings.json and regenerates the list.
