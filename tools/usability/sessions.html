Plan My Yard Sessions

Five homeowners, seven tasks, watched without helping. Write down where each person stops, and the words they did not know. These notes decide whether the app is finished.

Is it finished?

Finished when four of five people do tasks 1, 2, 6 and 7 unaided, and no task fails for the same reason twice.

Before each session

  1. A Windows laptop at 1366 × 768, with the newest release installed fresh (not updated over an old copy).
  2. An internet connection, and the person's own address, or a friend's.
  3. Advanced tools off (View menu). That is how a new copy starts.
  4. Screen recording, only if they agree. 45 minutes.
  5. Say: "We are testing the app, not you. Please think aloud." Then read one task at a time, and do not help.
  6. Write no name or address on this page. "Person 3" is enough; the notes are shared.
