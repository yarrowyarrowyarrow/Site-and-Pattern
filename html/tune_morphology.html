Photographs

An unsorted photo is one the catalogue already ships and nobody has looked at. Say what it is and the counters on the left start meaning something — until then "323 with a photo" is 323 pictures of we-don't-know-what.

An empty slot can find candidates: the species' wider openly-licensed set on iNaturalist, usually about a dozen, one of which is often the whole-plant shot the catalogue has none of. Needs internet.

Every import of your own is downscaled and has its EXIF stripped — a photo of your own yard carries your home's GPS coordinates, and these get committed.
