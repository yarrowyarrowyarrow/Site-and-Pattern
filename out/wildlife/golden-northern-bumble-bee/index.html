Golden Northern Bumble Bee

Bombus fervidus native bee

A long-tongued social bumble bee. Nests usually on the surface or above ground in deserted mouse nests, tall grass or haystacks. Forages on Astragalus, Cirsium, Helianthus, Lonicera and others.
