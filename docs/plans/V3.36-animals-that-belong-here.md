# V3.36 · Animals that belong here: the count

*The owner, while picking photographs: "Is there any other work you can do on
your end … I'm happy to run any other scripts that would be useful for
retrieving data that would help the app."*

The series the owner chose in V3.33 had **animals that belong here** next after
photos (F190, F249, and F172's bird colours). This increment builds the one
thing that needs the owner's computer, the count; the rule that uses it comes
when the count is back.

## What is wrong, and what is missing

- **F190.** Animals carry no range. The three ptarmigans eat bearberry, bog
  blueberry and alpine bistort, which grow in yards too, so they were drawn in
  28% of simulated designs (V2.97). Plants gained a locality check in V2.85
  (`site_fit`); animals have nothing like it.
- **F249.** The Overview names animals as examples (decision O4), and should
  name the ones an Edmonton homeowner knows: chickadees, not Rock Ptarmigan.
- **What the repo already has**: `data/fetched/fauna_nativity.json` holds
  Alberta and Saskatchewan record counts for all 1,144 animals (V2.61). It
  cannot tell a mountain bird from a backyard one: the White-tailed Ptarmigan
  has **1,955** Alberta records and the Willow Ptarmigan 288, all far from
  Edmonton; the Black-capped Chickadee has 589,199.
- **What is missing** is one number an animal: its records **within the
  region pack's circle** (`data/regions/edmonton/region.json`: 50 km of
  downtown, the same circle the Edmonton plant list uses).

## The count (this increment)

`scripts/fetch_animals_near.py`, run on the owner's PC:

- one GBIF occurrence count per animal per pack (`limit=0`, so no records are
  downloaded), with GBIF's circle filter `geoDistance`; zoo animals (living
  specimens) and fossils left out, as the province counts did;
- GBIF's machinery from the ecoregion seeder (its throttle, its 429 backoff,
  its rule that **a failure is not an absence**), loaded once;
- `--probe` first: four animals counted by the circle and by its box, so a
  circle filter GBIF ignored shows as a circle count larger than its box;
- about twenty minutes for Edmonton; resumable; `data/fetched/animals_near.json`.

## What the count will be used for (next)

Not decided until the numbers are seen, but the shape, so the owner knows what
they are running it for:

1. **A locality rule for animals** like `site_fit`'s for plants: recorded near
   the yard, recorded in the province but not near it, not recorded in either.
   P9 matters more here than for plants: few people record solitary bees, so
   none near Edmonton is *unknown* for a bee and close to *absent* for a bird
   eBird volunteers count every day. The rule will likely trust absence only
   for well-recorded groups (birds, mammals, butterflies).
2. **The 3D scene** stops drawing an animal well recorded in the province and
   never near the yard: the ptarmigans.
3. **A drafted list of about 30 familiar animals** (F249), ranked by records
   near Edmonton among the animals the catalogue's plants feed, for the owner
   to correct on a page, then used by the Overview's named examples.

## Could not verify

- That GBIF honours `geoDistance` with `limit=0` and repeated `basisOfRecord`;
  the probe checks the first against the box, and prints the counts.
- How many animals have no records near Edmonton because nobody looked; that
  is what the count will show.
