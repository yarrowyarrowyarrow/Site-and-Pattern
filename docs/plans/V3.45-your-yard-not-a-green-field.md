# V3.45 · Your yard, not a green field (3D-3, F262)

*The owner, 13 November 2026: "Continue with 3D-3 on V3.45, the recommendations are fine."*

The third of the eight increments in [the V3.43 review](V3.43-the-3d-preview-reviewed.md), which
is the handover and holds the findings, the guardrails and the decisions. This file is what 3D-3
found, decided and built.

## The decision this increment needed

| | Decision | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| D3 | Ground and houses where nothing is recorded | **Lawn inside the outline, pale outside; houses as plain massing, no invented windows or colours** ("the recommendations are fine", 13 November 2026) |

## What the review found, and what the code said

Findings 2 and 3, and the half of finding 1 that V3.43 left ("Buildings are solid boxes. Roofs
and walls are 3D-3's"):

- **Nothing drawn on the ground reached 3D.** `build_scene` read a `custom_shape` only when it
  had a height, so a bed, mulch, a lawn, a path, a patio, a driveway and a pond were all the one
  meadow texture that ran to the horizon. The review lot has seven drawn areas; the picture had
  none of them.
- **A hedge or a fence was not in the scene at all** (`hedgerow` features: the contract's own
  docstring listed them under "Not yet in the scene").
- **No street**, though the app has known the street side since V3.29 (the person's answer in
  Plan My Yard) and has kept the road lines of a scanned site with the design since V2.58
  (`frontage.roads_in_project`).
- **A house was a tan box** with a flat top the colour of its walls.

## What V3.45 built

**The scene says what the ground is** (`src/scene_site.py`, Qt-free, called by `build_scene`;
additive fields, no `SCENE_VERSION` bump):

- `ground`: `"pale"` for a yard, `"meadow"` for a walk through a wild community, which is a
  meadow and is drawn as it always was. A scene without the field draws meadow, as before.
- `surfaces[]`, bottom first: the outline's inside as lawn (D3), then every drawn area by its
  kind. One vocabulary with `yard_overview.BED_SHAPES` / `NOT_PLANTED`, `shape_tool.AREA_PRESETS`
  and `lawn_zones.ZONE_TYPES`: a test fails on a shape type with no surface kind, and another on
  a kind the viewer has no look for. A *Custom* shape, and any type the table does not know,
  keeps the person's colour.

  | The person draws | It is drawn as |
  |---|---|
  | Garden Bed | soil |
  | Mulch Area | wood-chip mulch |
  | Lawn Area, Lawn — to convert | lawn (it is lawn today, whatever is planned for it) |
  | Pathway | gravel |
  | Patio / Deck | paving |
  | Water Feature | water |
  | Restoration — Year 1 | prepared ground under straw |
  | Restoration — Year 3 | a young meadow |
  | Established native, Existing remnant | meadow |
  | Custom, or anything else | the colour the person gave it |

- `lines[]`: hedge, fence, living fence, windbreak, with the width the design holds. **Nothing
  records a height for a line**, so each kind has one estimate (as V3.43's fruit has a size by
  form), said in the module: a fence 1.2 m, a hedge 1.2 m, a living fence 1.6 m, a windbreak 3 m.
- `street`: a pale sidewalk and road on the street side, **only where the side is known**. Told
  by the person: a sidewalk where V3.43's street view stands the neighbour (4 m out) and a road
  beyond it, both schematic. Measured from the road lines kept with the design: those lines,
  drawn where they are and as wide as their class, and the sidewalk only where it lies clear of
  them. Where the side is only assumed (south, with nothing to go on) no street is drawn: the
  Overview says "taken to be the south side" in words, and a road in the picture would say it as
  fact (P9). None either for a yard with no outline: nothing says where it ends.
- `buildings[].roof`: a hint for plain massing. A gable only on a clean four-corner footprint of
  house size, its ridge along the long side and **at the recorded height** (the walls stop lower),
  so the building is never taller than the shade map's; anything else gets a flat roof. Nothing
  records a roof, a pitch, a window or a colour, and none of the last two is drawn.

**The viewer draws them** (`html/scene3d/02c-site.js`, a new chunk loaded after `02-plants.js`,
which also takes the outline and the buildings out of that file, at 687 of 700 lines and now at
635):

- Surfaces as flat polygons draped on the ground, each kind its own quiet procedural texture.
  **Texture, never geometry** (V2.29's lesson): no blades of grass, no chips of mulch. Where there
  is terrain each triangle is cut in four, the same way for all of them so neighbours share their
  new corners and no crack opens, until the surface follows the slope.
- Each kind has a place in the pile (a path over a bed over the lawn), which is also its depth
  offset, so two surfaces millimetres apart do not flicker at a distance.
- Fences as posts and three rails; hedges, living fences and windbreaks as one plain green mass
  each, narrower at the top.
- The sidewalk and the road, pale.
- Houses: light walls, a darker roof with an overhang.
- Winter: snow lies on the soft ground and on roofs; paths, paving and the street read as packed
  snow; water as ice.

Nothing here runs per frame: everything is built once per scene push.

**A drawn line keeps what the person set** (found on the way, below).

## Corrections made while building

- **The street is drawn only where its side is known.** The handover said "a pale sidewalk and
  road on the street side". On a design whose street is only assumed that would have drawn a road
  along the south edge of every yard nobody had said anything about.
- **The chunk is `02c-site.js`, not a 28th.** After `02-plants.js`, beside the ground it draws on
  and the two builders it took over. `27-views.js` stays the viewer's last chunk, so no probe had
  to learn a new one to wait for.
- **Colours, twice.** The first lawn was a vivid green that read as plastic, the first soil too
  red, and the pale ground nearly white from above. All three were quietened after looking.
- **The fence's top rail stood above its posts** (1.34 m on a 1.28 m post). Found by the gate's
  own measurement, not by eye.
- **A hedge was a square slab**, and a 3 m windbreak read as a wall. Each stretch narrows to 0.6
  of its width at the top.
- **A drawn line's width, spacing and colour were thrown away when it was saved.** Draw › Shape
  asks them and the map draws the line with them, but the map's callback when the line is finished
  carries only its points, species, style and length, and the handler wrote 1.5 m, 1.0 m and green
  whatever had been set. A 3 m windbreak came back 1.5 m wide the next time the design was opened.
  Not a 3D fault, and older than this programme; found because 3D now draws the width. The
  settings are stashed when the tool is armed (as a drawn tree canopy's foliage has been since
  V2.13) and read when the line is finished. **A line saved before V3.45 stays as it was saved.**

## What is left alone

- **Where the ground is not recorded it stays plain.** No invented driveway, no neighbour's
  lawn, no kerb. The example yard, which holds an outline and plants and no drawn bed, is plants
  on a lawn: that is what the design says.
- **The walker is not stopped by a fence or a hedge.** Buildings stop him (V3.43); lines do not
  yet.
- **The shade map** does not read lines either, and is not changed here. A gabled house casts a
  little less shade in 3D than the shade map's box of the same height does: the map is the one
  the planner reads.
- **A yard with no outline has no lawn and no street.** Its beds lie on pale ground.
- Seasons for plants and the shade deaths said out loud (3D-4), the planner and shade (3D-5),
  light (3D-6), the trees and herbs themselves (3D-7).

## What was run

- **The whole suite**, with every optional dependency and CI's font
  (`scripts/run_tests.py --exclude tests.test_undo_redo --max-skips 25`): **5,765 run, 0 failed,
  12 skipped**, against 5,721 on V3.44's last commit (31 in `test_scene_site`, 10 in
  `test_yard_render`, 3 in `test_slope_render`). That run was of the commit before the line
  settings; on the final tree the thirteen modules that touch a line, the main window or the
  viewer were run again (410 tests, none failed, none skipped), with the five new ones in
  `test_line_settings`, the architecture guard and the modules that read these documents.
- **The whole-yard gate** (`tests/test_yard_render.py`, the real viewer in headless Chromium):
  33 checks pass. **Nine fail on V3.44's viewer** (`YARD_VIEWER_ROOT=<a V3.44 checkout>/html`):
  every drawn area, the bed's colour, the custom colour, lawn inside and pale outside, the pile's
  order, the street, the roof, the fence and hedge, and the snow. The tenth new check is the one
  that must pass on both: a scene from before keeps its meadow and its boxes.
- **On a slope** (`tests/test_slope_render.py`, 6 m of relief): a surface lies within a few
  centimetres of the ground along its whole length, a fence's posts and a hedge stand on it, and
  the ground outside the outline is the pale one. 18 pass.
- **The line settings** (`tests/test_line_settings.py`): three of the five fail on the handler as
  it was.
- **Looked at**, not only measured: the two contact sheets above; a yard holding one of every
  kind of area, in July, October and January; a measured road beside a yard; a flat roof on an
  L-shaped footprint; the review lot from 60 m up.
- `tests/test_undo_redo.py` is excluded, as in every container run (CLAUDE.md): it needs a real
  desktop.

## Not verified

- **A real graphics card, Windows, macOS and frame rate.** The gates and the sheets are headless
  Chromium drawing in software. The surfaces add a few flat meshes and no per-frame work; the
  readout (Ctrl+Shift+F) is there for the owner's laptop.
- **Flicker between overlapping surfaces on a real depth buffer.** Each kind has its own depth
  offset and a few millimetres of lift, and none showed in the software renders from the ground,
  from above or from 60 m. A graphics card's depth precision differs.
- **A real scanned site's road lines.** The road ribbon was drawn from a line typed in by hand;
  no session can reach OpenStreetMap to scan one.
- **The line settings through the real map page.** The fix is tested at the two controllers, with
  the map's callback handed over as the page sends it. Drawing a line with the mouse was not done.
