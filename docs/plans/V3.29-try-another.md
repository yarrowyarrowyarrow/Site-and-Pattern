## The plan, before the work

- **PY-8 first**, because the bar and the variant both need it. Every message
  the generator leaves gets a **code** beside its English (`generation_notes`,
  a list of `{code, …}` parallel to `generation_warnings`, which stays the
  advanced Design notes and the CLI's output, unchanged). `src/plan_notes.py`
  (Qt-free) is the one table from code to the simple app's words, or marks a
  code advanced-only; a test reads every code the generator can emit and fails
  on one the table does not know. *About this plan* is at most six lines and
  two *could not* lines, its numbers from the functions the Overview uses.
- **PY-6.** `variant` on `generate_design_offline`, the facade, the CLI and
  the MCP tool (optional, default 0, so variant 0 is today's plan exactly).
  A variant rotates the ranked pool **within each band and layer** (so a
  variant swaps a top-pick shrub for another top-pick shrub, never for a worse
  plant), shifts the anchor grid's phase and the layouts' seeds. After a plan
  lands a strip above the map says *Your plan: N plants of K kinds* with *Try
  another*, *About this plan* and *Undo*; in the simple app it replaces the
  modal Design notes box.
- **PY-7.** `setting` (front / back) and `street_side` on the generator,
  remembered in `site_config` (`yard_setting`, `street_side`); the person's
  street side wins over the measured and the assumed in `frontage`. *Front*:
  the band 1 m deep along the street side holds only plants 60 cm or shorter
  that stay put, planted as repeated drifts of one or two showy low plants; no
  meadow mix, no brush pile. *Back*: the meadow mix and at most two structures
  (a brush pile, a bee hotel).

Planning takes 5–10 s a plan here, so *Try another* says *Planning another…*
and greys its buttons meanwhile.

**The fill changed, and the V3.28 numbers with it.** Variant 1's first build
hit the 300-plant ceiling at 58% cover: its fillers were the plan's most
numerous plants, and those were violets 22 cm across, 64 of which added 2 m².
The fill now takes the **widest** herbaceous plants among the plan's own and
the pool's first twelve, none under 40 cm (`plan_cover.MIN_SPREAD_M`), and
only herbaceous ones (`FILLER_TYPES`): the first try at widest-first filled a
yard with 4.5 m chokecherries. *Fill it now* is now 248 plants at 70% (267 at
67% in V3.28), *Over two or three summers* 92 at 40%; every probe rule still
holds, and the bed-with-no-outline case went from 12 plants to 20.

**About this plan** for the plain plan reads:

> **Your plan: 248 plants of 10 kinds**
> In your yard outline.
> In flower from March to October. Only one kind in April and October.
> Recorded as food for 170 kinds of bee, 119 of butterfly and moth and 15 of
> bird, such as the American Robin.
> About $2,124–$4,303 at Edmonton native nurseries.

The flowering and food lines are the Overview's own (`yard_overview_items`),
computed on the plan's plants alone.

## Decisions made on the way

- **The modal Design notes box is gone in both apps**, not only the simple
  one: the advanced notes are listed under *About this plan* instead, one
  press away and non-modal. Part 07 asked only that the simple app lose it;
  a box between a person and the plan they asked for is no better with the
  advanced tools on.
- **The notes stay with the design** (`generation_warnings` and
  `generation_notes` copied to the project's properties), so *About this
  plan* still answers after a save and reopen.
- **A front yard has no meadow mix**, and its tallest plants trade places
  with short ones from the back half (`street_band.grade`), the tallest
  nearest the street first. A plant with a vine at its foot stays, so the
  vine keeps its host. That is what lifts the cues-to-care tally: the band
  alone did not move *height graded*.
- **A brush pile now keeps planting off itself** (`exclusion`), wherever it
  came from: a back-yard plan seats it in the most open spot, then plants
  around it.
- **The person's street side wins** over the measured and the assumed in
  `frontage`, so the Overview's street line says "the east side, as you said".
- **The MCP tool is unchanged**: it runs the AI path only, which has no
  variant. The CLI's `generate --no-llm` takes `--variant`, `--setting` and
  `--street-side`.

## Deliberately left

- **Height grading measures north–south** (`cues_to_care` reads latitude),
  so a front yard whose street is east is graded toward the street by the
  plan but judged north–south by the cue. A known limit of the cue, older
  than this increment.
- **The fill can still reach the plant ceiling** on a yard whose best
  herbaceous plants are all small; it then says so under *What I could not
  do*.
- **No "Shrubs at the back" sentence**: part 07's example claimed it; nothing
  measures it, so the summary does not say it.
