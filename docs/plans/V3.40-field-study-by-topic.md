## What was there

`field_study.generate_quiz` alternated two kinds of question for five
questions: a photograph to name, from the whole catalogue (whichever photos
happened to be cached, with eight more fetched per quiz), and "which plant
does this specialist depend on", from ten animals. One design-gap question
went first when the design had a gap. No choice of anything.

## What changes

`src/quiz_topics.py` (Qt-free, deterministic, every source injectable)
sorts the questions by what you are practising:

| Group | Topic | What it asks | Pool (measured) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Name the plant** | Wildflowers | a photograph, four wildflower names | 244 (wildflowers, herbs, groundcovers) |
| | Grasses, sedges and rushes | a photograph, four of the same kind | 80 |
| | Trees and shrubs | | 74 |
| | Any plant | four of the plant's own type | 424 |
| **Who uses whom** | What caterpillars eat | "Which of these plants do Mourning Cloak caterpillars eat?" | 65 butterflies and moths with an English name and a recorded host |
| | Birds and their fruit and seeds | "Which of these is the Cedar Waxwing recorded eating?" | 35 birds |
| | Bees and their flowers | "Which of these flowers is the … bee recorded at?" | 53 bees |
| | Animals that depend on a few plants | the old "specialist" question, in plain words | 10 animals |
| **When and where** | When it flowers | "Which of these flowers in April?" | 418 plants with a flowering time |
| | Where it grows | "Which of these grows in dry ground in full sun?" | every plant |
| | The landscape you walked | which plants grow there; "Pull the Chokecherry, and which of these animals loses the only plant there it is recorded with?"; its food-web gap | the walk you planted in |

and *A bit of everything*, the old quiz, kept as a choice.

- **Plants:** every plant; native around the region's centre (the reviewed
  list, `native_here`); or one of the walks' seven communities. The
  landscape topic is about the walk and ignores it.
- **Length:** 5, 10 or 20 questions; 10 to start.
- **A wrong answer is never secretly right.** A distractor is never a plant
  the animal is recorded using in that way; for flowering and growing it is a
  plant recorded as flowering at another time or needing other ground.
  "Recorded", in every question about an animal: the records say an animal
  was seen at a plant somewhere in two provinces (V3.20).
- **Fair company:** a grass among grasses, a shrub among shrubs, wherever the
  pool allows.
- **Animals by their English names, one species each** (WD-7). The first
  draft asked about "The Mining Bees", which is *Andrena spp.*, eight groups
  of which the catalogue records at genus level; and about "Forest Tent
  Caterpillar caterpillars", a moth named for its young, which now reads
  "the Forest Tent Caterpillar".
- **One question per animal or plant:** a twenty-question draft asked about
  the Yellow Warbler four times, each with another of its plants.
- **Honest when it cannot:** a topic that cannot fill the quiz makes fewer
  and says so ("Only 7 questions could be made on this topic from these
  plants"); one that can make none says why (no photographs on this computer
  yet; walk a landscape first; too few plants in this focus), and never
  stands another topic's questions in.
- **Photographs:** the screen says how many of the topic's photographs are
  on this computer; the warmer fetches the chosen topic's first, forty at a
  time (eight random ones from the whole catalogue before).
- **Best score per topic and plants**, kept in the progress table the walk
  writes to (`quiz_best:<topic>:<focus>`), by share and then by count, shown
  under the choices and after a quiz.

## Not changed

- No difficulty levels (the V3.38 plan): topics, plants and length first.
- `field_study.generate_quiz` and its scripting use are unchanged; *A bit of
  everything* is that quiz.
- The quiz's look: the question, photograph, answers and explanation are drawn
  as before.

## Verified, and not

- `tests/test_quiz_topics.py` holds each rule on a made-up catalogue; the
  widget's tests play a topic to the end on the real seeded catalogue and
  keep a best score; every topic and plants choice was built on the real
  catalogue in a session and read (a sample of each is in the commit's
  session).
- Not seen on a real desktop: the choosers' look at the Learn window's width,
  and how quickly the photographs arrive for a topic on a home connection.
