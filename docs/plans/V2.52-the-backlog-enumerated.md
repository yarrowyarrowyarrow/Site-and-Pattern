# V2.52 — the backlog, enumerated; and the ID that collided three times

> *"Review the Roadmap_next.md and lets figure out next steps. I'd like a new
> list of all the features we have not yet added that I can choose from."*

A reasonable ask that the documents could not answer. This increment produces the
list, and repairs the four things that stopped it existing already.

**Nothing is built here.** No source changes, no schema bump. The list is the
deliverable; the pick is the author's.

---

## 1. Why the question was hard to answer

`ROADMAP_NEXT.md` is 1,052 lines and roughly 60% of it is a shipped record. The
unbuilt entries are spread across eight lettered themes inside it, plus
`PHILOSOPHY_ROADMAP.md` (F1–F62), plus `ROADMAP.md` (the legacy X/P/V ledger),
plus `USER_FEEDBACK.md`, plus the tails of `SPRITE_AUDIT.md` and `DATA_GAPS.md`.

Answering "what is left?" therefore means reading six documents and cross-checking
them against each other — and when I did that, they disagreed. Four entries listed
as open had shipped. Two were half-built with nothing saying which half. One
feature was described twice under two IDs. And the ID sequence had collided
again.

The fix is a seventh document, which needs justifying, because this project's own
roadmap names sprawl as a weakness. The justification: `BACKLOG.md` holds **one
line per unbuilt item and nothing else**, and its stated rule is that a row leaves
when it ships. It is an index over documents that keep their reasoning. The
alternative — a fifty-row table at the top of a 1,052-line file that is mostly
history — is the arrangement that produced this problem.

---

## 2. The ID has now collided three times, and the second one was flagged

`ROADMAP_NEXT.md:8` read **"Next free ID: F111."** F111 and F112 both shipped in
V2.48.

Four IDs had two claimants:

| ID | Claimant A | Claimant B |
|---|---|---|
| **F90** | Plant directory — shipped V2.41 | Design variants — unbuilt |
| **F108** | Life-size creatures + flap-glide + collision — V2.46 | Flower colour as a filter — V2.47 |
| **F109** | Three verbs in the 3D preview + the net — V2.46 | The plant directory as a public website — V2.47 |
| **F110** | Bee mode at the right scale — V2.46 | Flower colour per species — V2.48 |

F90 is the interesting one, because **it was caught and written down and then not
acted on**. `docs/plans/V2.43-learn-and-design-two-doors.md:392`, under "Docs to
update", says: *"`docs/ROADMAP_NEXT.md` (next free ID is **F95**; also flag that
**F90 is double-assigned** — plant directory *and* design variants)"*. Neither
edit was made. Three increments later, with the pointer still reading F111, V2.46
and V2.47 reached for F108–F110 independently and both were right to, because the
document told them the range was free.

**The lesson is not "be more careful."** It is that the next-free-ID was a
*sentence*, and a sentence is not a data structure. It was wrong twice out of
three times because updating it is a step you can complete a release without
noticing you skipped. So V2.52 replaces it with an **ID ledger table** covering
F63–F119, claimant and status per row, and the pointer is now derived from the
table rather than asserted beside it. A hole (F95) is a row that says "free"; a
collision is a row that says so.

Resolution follows the rule V2.37 set — a shipped assignment is kept, because it
is in the commit history, so only an unbuilt claimant may move:

- **F90 is the plant directory.** Design variants becomes **F113**.
- **F108/F109/F110 have two *shipped* claimants each** and neither can move. They
  are disambiguated by release in the ledger. Ugly, and honest; inventing a
  renumbering would falsify two sets of commit messages.
- **F114–F119** assigned to the six 3D-fidelity leftovers that had been carried as
  prose in `SPRITE_AUDIT.md` and Theme H with no handle at all. Next free: **F120**.

## 3. What the code said that the roadmap did not

Every status in `BACKLOG.md` was checked against the tree. Six disagreements:

**F77 · the neighbour's-eye view — shipped V2.33, still listed open.**
`src/presentation_still.py:37-39` declares
`CAMERA_PRESETS = ("overview", "orbit", "walk", "sidewalk")` and the comment
directly above it says *"`sidewalk` is new here and is the roadmap's F77"*. The
camera is `html/scene3d/08-modes.js:sidewalk`. The V2.33 entry in this very
document predicted it — *"a sidewalk preset that is **F77** for free"* — and
nobody came back to strike the card off. Nineteen releases.

**F62 · layer aspect axis — shipped V2.33 as F65.** `ROADMAP_NEXT.md`'s own Theme
A table says "(was F62)". `PHILOSOPHY_ROADMAP.md` still listed it open *and* the
"still deferred" paragraph still deferred it.

**F60 · blade-class axis on tree archetypes — absorbed by F64, V2.33.** Marked ◐
"poplar/aspen split done, rest open", and also deferred in the same paragraph as
F62.

**F12 · citations — half shipped, and the shipped half is not the half the card
was about.** `src/citations.py` landed in V2.42 with a real formatter and two
honesty rules (a `NOT_A_WORK` placeholder is never dressed up; every entry reads
`unverified` because it was transcribed rather than checked). It is rendered by
`plant_directory_window._citation_block` and by Help → Data Sources. It reaches
**no design-side surface** — not the Analysis panel, not the relationship web,
not the 3D dossier. A user building a design still cannot tell a sourced record
from an invention without leaving for the directory. The formatter takes a key
and every `Edge` already carries `source`, so what is left is wiring.

**F73 · "in my yard, on this date" — no longer a schema bump.** `taken_on` is on
`plant_photos` (`src/db/photos.py:105`, `schema.sql:754`). UI work.

**F20 · maintenance-over-time curve — delivered in substance by F42.**
`maintenance_calendar` ships 36–76 → 18–38 → 9–19 → 6–11 hours a year with a test
that fails if the curve flattens. Only a *drawing* of it is missing, which belongs
inside F42's tab, not as its own card.

And one duplication: **F85 and F106 are the same feature.** Both propose a fauna
companion that explains the app; both prescribe "do the cheap glossary half first,
and separately"; both cite `docent.py`'s beat shape; both flag the same P11 risk in
the same words. F85 survives (older handle, and the one `USER_FEEDBACK.md` points
at); F106 is retired in place with its text kept as the record.

**The glossary half is still uncalled**, on the third increment to write it down.
`src/climate.py:96 zone_description()` returns exactly the right sentence for a
hardiness zone and has **no caller anywhere in `src/`** — verified, not inferred.
There is no glossary module in the tree. ~12 of ~20 Site Info metrics have no
explanation of any kind.

## 4. What the roadmap looks like when you count it

Two things fell out of the enumeration that were not visible one theme at a time.

**The self-assessment has moved once in nineteen releases.** `ROADMAP_NEXT.md`
carries a list of five things the app is not good at yet. Scored in V2.52: "it
looks like a diagram" is largely fixed; the photographs are **unmoved** (111 of 434
plants with none, 0 species with a habit shot, 62 of 69 bees with none — the
*structure* landed in V2.35/36 and the *content* did not); the beauty argument,
the professional workflow and the sprawl are unbuilt. The sprawl has grown: ten
buttons on the 3D toolbar now, not nine.

**Where the work went.** V2.43–V2.46 were Learn-side and 3D-creature work.
V2.47–V2.51 were website, catalogue and data work. **The design side proper has
not had an increment since V2.42.** This is recorded as context, not as a
complaint — the website work came straight from the author, and the depth is what
makes the app worth opening. But this document has twice accused itself of exactly
this drift, and writing the accusation a third time without the numbers beside it
would be the least useful of the available options.

Eleven releases — **V2.37 through V2.46, and V2.50** — had shipped without ever
reaching the Shipped section. Their record lived only in `docs/plans/`, and for
the Learn-side ones in a single prose sentence under Theme H. A Shipped section
with holes is worse than none, because the holes are invisible. They are now a
release-ledger table, one row each, linked to their plans.

## 5. What was deliberately not done

- **No feature was built.** The ask was for a list to choose from; building
  something first would have chosen for the author.
- **F108/F109/F110 were not renumbered.** Both claimants shipped. Renumbering
  either falsifies commit history for the sake of a tidier table.
- **The legacy `ROADMAP.md` was not rewritten**, only corrected in four places
  (P1 and V5 → Done; P4 and P5 flagged as retirement candidates). It is explicitly
  a historical document and rewriting history is not repair.
- **P4 and P5 were flagged, not deleted.** Retiring a card is the author's call;
  the flag makes it a decision rather than a drift.
- **The counts in `BACKLOG.md` group J are carried forward**, not re-measured —
  111/434 photos and the 24 unverified bird rows come from `DATA_GAPS.md` and
  `SPRITE_AUDIT.md`. Re-measuring them is part of whichever data job gets picked.
- **No recommendation was promoted into a decision.** `BACKLOG.md` ends with a
  three-item recommendation clearly marked as one.
