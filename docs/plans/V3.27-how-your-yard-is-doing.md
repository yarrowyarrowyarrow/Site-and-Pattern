## What was built

**The core** (`src/yard_overview.py`, `src/yard_overview_items.py`, Qt-free).
`yard_overview` measures on the planting area V3.20 defined: `cover` (the
union of each placed plant's mature-spread disc, clipped to the area),
`bare_ground`, `fill_points` (a staggered 60 cm grid over the bare ground),
`fill_plan` (which low plants, how many, what cost) and `yard_parts` (PY-12).
`yard_overview_items.overview(project)` returns the six items in O7's order,
each with a status word, its lines, one action and, where part 02 asks, a
*More detail* link. Every number is computed by the function the advanced page
and the PDF use: food counts by `what_it_feeds.food_counts`, flowering months
by `month_by_month`, nativity by `design_native`, street cues by
`cues_to_care`, plant and mulch cost by `sourcing`, hours by
`build_maintenance_calendar`, water by `water_budget`. Tests read each source
and assert the overview says the same number.

**The worked example, measured** (19 plants, 11 × 8 m, central Edmonton), 254
words in 0.2 s:

| Item | Status | Says |
|---|---|---|
| Food for wildlife | Looks good | Recorded as food for 182 kinds of bee, 167 of butterfly and moth and 19 of bird, such as the Cedar Waxwing. |
| Flowers through the season | Worth a look | In flower from March to September. Nothing in October, and only one kind in April. |
| Right plants for here | Looks good | All 14 kinds of plant are native around Edmonton. |
| How full it will be | Worth a look | about a third of the area you outlined (31 of 88 m²) |
| How it will look from the street | Worth a look | 2 of 5 signs of care: a clear edge and tall plants at the back. Next: a mown strip (taken to be the south side). |
| What it will take | To plan for | Plants about $203–$406 at Edmonton native nurseries; mulch 4–7 m³ (often free; $154–$528 bought); first year 36–76 hours and about 130 L a week; once filled in 6–11 hours a year. |

Part 06 §6.1 measured 188 / 180 / 21 and "12 of 14 native" before the
example's Showy Milkweed and Canada Goldenrod were replaced (R14). The rule is
the same; the counts are today's catalogue's.

**The page** (`src/yard_overview_page.py`, `src/yard_overview_flow.py`). Design
opens on *Overview*, first and current; in the simple app it replaces the
Report card, which the Advanced tools switch now holds. It refills when shown
and after any edit, open or undo (`follow_design.request_sync` pokes it). Each
card's button does what its words say: *Add a berry shrub* and *Find an October
flower* set the plant list's filters; *Draw a mown strip*, *the bed's edge* or
*a path* open Draw › Shape on that type; *Ways to fill the gaps* and *Swap*
open their dialogs; *Print the shopping list and care plan* runs Export PDF;
*Find my address* and *Outline my yard* go to the pin and the outline tool;
the empty page offers Plan my yard. **MD-4**: a *More detail* link turns the
Advanced tools on, shows the page that explains the item (Food, Over time) and
says once a session how to turn them off.

**Fill the gaps** (OV-9). The bare ground, then three equal answers (O3):
plant low plants 60 cm apart (the design's own grasses, sedges, groundcovers or
wildflowers under 60 cm, most numerous first, else the region's two
best-ranked grasses or sedges by PR-1), with the count and cost said before
anything is placed; keep the gaps mulched; or outline only the beds. Planting
is one undo step through the area-fill path. On the example the fill brings
cover from 31% to 79%, which turns the card *Looks good*. **Part 06 expected
90%**: that assumed each filler spreads at least its 60 cm spacing, and the
example's low plants are recorded narrower. The acceptance is the card's
status, which holds.

**Swap for a local native** (OV-10). `substitution.substitutes` gained
`native_only` and `site_config`: candidates are native at the pin (the region's
list inside a pack, the province elsewhere). The dialog names each with its
flowering months and where it is sold; *Swap* replaces every instance where it
stands, one undo step, through `ProjectStore` and a map redraw.

**The PDF** (OV-12). The first page's *Design Summary* block, with its
Habitat Value Score and "value vs. price" line, is now the overview's six
items in the same words. A block that would run off the page starts the next
one. No score (O1); the score is still on the advanced Report card and
Habitat pages, unchanged.

**WD-7.** An animal is named only by an English name: 820 of 1,144 store their
Latin name as the common name and three a generic one ("A Leafcutter Bee").
`example_animal` picks a bird that eats the seed or fruit, else a butterfly or
moth, else a bumble bee, most of the design's plants first, and reads a pack's
`overview.familiar_animals` first once F249's list exists.

**OV-13.** The pack's `overview.price_note` ("at Edmonton native nurseries",
written in V3.24 for this) is the cost line's tail; `overview.street_rule` is
read but Edmonton's pack has none (O5). A place with a rule shows it under the
street item.

**PY-12.** `yard_parts` splits the planting area by a line through the
largest building's middle, across the way to the street (the road when stored,
else the south side, and `assumed` says which). A lot with its house reads
"Front yard about 170 m², back yard about 180 m²." under the cover item. It is
not yet fed to the "too big for this yard" words, whose area is still the
outline's, or to Plan My Yard, which is increment 11's.

**The API.** `permadesign_api.yard_overview(project)` returns the items and the
text, so the overview can be read without the window (contract updated).

## Deliberately left

- **The flowering strip is a line of text** ("Apr 1   May 5 …"), not a drawn
  bar; it reads to a screen reader as a sentence.
- **A card that looks good has no action** where part 06 offered "Show the bare
  ground": that needs a map overlay of its own.
- **Height grading and a showy repeat have no button**: nothing draws them, so
  the card says the next step in words.
- **The page scrolls at 1366 × 768**: with the example the six cards are 960 px
  tall in a 659 px view (measured offscreen), against part 06's estimate of
  570. Each card is whole and readable; shortening them is a
  wording pass for the owner to look at.
- **F249**, the list of about 30 animals familiar in Edmonton yards, is a data
  job; the code reads it when it exists.

## What CI caught

The first building push went red on one test: in CI's DejaVu Sans the
Overview scrolled 11 px sideways. A card's *More detail* link and its button
shared a row, and the longest pair ("See what flowers when ▸" beside "Find an
October flower") needed 371 px where the panel can give 300. This container
draws in Inter, preferred system-wide, so the suite passed here.
`tests/fonts/dejavu.conf` reproduces CI's font (`FONTCONFIG_FILE=...`); with it
the old page fails and the fixed one, link and button on lines of their own,
passes. The test now also asserts every card fits 300 px, which holds in either
font.
