## What was there

`src/lesson_track.py`: four steps (keystone plants, the food web, succession,
ranges), one paragraph each, with a readout "against your design". In the
Learn window "your design" was the walk's saved sandbox, which most people
had not planted in, so the readouts said "nothing placed yet".

## What changes

**`src/lessons.py` holds every word** (D6: the owner's rewrite touches only
this file), as three lessons of short pages:

| Lesson | Pages |
|---|---|
| Designing for biodiversity | Some plants feed far more than others (keystone plants, and who Doug Tallamy is) · Plant, caterpillar, bird · One plant, many neighbours · Plants change over time · Places to shelter (leaves, bare ground, brush, snags; bee hotels' cleaning) · Ranges, not certainties |
| Site factors to consider in design | Sun and shade · Water · Soil · Slope · Wind and snow · Frost and cold · What is already there (and Click Before You Dig) |
| Aesthetic considerations | Three ways to lay a bed out (wild, structured, themed) · Height · Layers and filling the ground · Drifts and repetition · Colour through the year · Showing it is on purpose (Nassauer's cues to care) |

Each page has two short paragraphs (67 to 143 words), a **Look at it**
pointer to a real place in the app (a page that only the advanced app shows
says so), its sources, and **a readout about a landscape the reader
chooses**: the one the window is about (named: "The walk you planted in:
Aspen Parkland grove", or "This design" in the main window), any of the seven
walks, or one of the eight newest saved designs. The readouts are computed
from the landscape: which of its plants are recorded as food for the most
caterpillars, whether both ends of the food web are there, how many of its
animals hang on one plant, how long its plants take to reach their size, the
shelter placed, its plants' sun and water needs, the frost dates for its pin,
what was marked as already there, heights, layers, how often each plant is
repeated, and the months with something in flower.

**What the drafting found, and changed:**

- The draft said a willow "can be food for a hundred kinds of caterpillar".
  The catalogue records about fifteen for a willow, an aspen or a chokecherry
  (Tallamy's hundreds are his eastern counts), and the walk's own card says
  so on the next screen. The text compares instead ("a dozen or more, where
  many garden flowers are food for none") and the readout gives the
  landscape's real numbers.
- The time readout used `succession`'s roles and said the Aspen Parkland
  had "0 that take years and last", because that model calls aspen a
  pioneer, which it is in a forest's history and is not in a yard's first
  summers. The readout counts years to full size instead (`years_to_maturity`).
- "Most bees here nest in the ground" was checked against the catalogue: 40
  of the 45 bees recorded with a nest of their own nest in the ground.
- MacIvor & Packer (2015), on bee hotels, was added to `docs/REFERENCES.md`.

**Rules the text keeps** (`tests/test_lessons.py`): no place typed in (the
region guard's rule; `{place}` is filled from the pack); none of the
plant-use words the example tool refuses (P12), and the lessons, the quiz and
the walk's cards joined the screen-text P12 scan; none of the glossary's
"never" words; none of a list of stock phrases that read as machine-written
("delve", "journey", "vibrant" …) and no em dashes; every source written out
and in the bibliography.

The end of a lesson leads to the next ("Next lesson →"); the end of the last
starts again.

## Not changed

- `src/lesson_track.py` and the scripting API's `lesson_track(project)`: the
  API's contract is frozen, and nothing else reads it now.
- No progress kept between sessions (BACKLOG F88 notes the old widget lost
  its step on every refresh; this one keeps the lesson and page while the
  window is open, which is all a first draft needs).
- The **Look at it** pointers are text, not buttons: the Learn window has no
  design open to send them to.
