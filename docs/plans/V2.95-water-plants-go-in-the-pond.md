# V2.95 — Water plants go in the pond

*One item: **F185**, left by V2.90 as a design rule and numbered by V2.91. The
owner said "continue" after V2.94, and this was the alternative offered twice.
F187 (the ground bounce) is the owner's call and was not taken.*

## What was wrong

Measured through the real generator: a stub AI client returns a fixed spec, then
`generate_design` runs with no network and no zoning, and `build_scene` is asked
where each water plant ended up. Water here means one of the 23 species
`zoning.needs_standing_water` names (V2.91).

| The spec asked for | What the generator did |
|---|---|
| A pond, two Yellow Pond-lilies, three Cattails, a Sago Pondweed | Every water plant on dry ground, 9 to 28 m from the pond; 0 of 6 in water |
| Pond-lilies and arrowheads, no pond | Placed on dry ground; no note |
| A pond, no water plants | A bare pond |
| A pond and the seeded "Pond & Aquatic Edge" community | All eight water members on dry ground, 25 m from the pond |

Three reasons:

1. **The pond is placed last.** `_place_within_boundary` places plants, mixes,
   communities, then structures, each structure on the best cell left. A water
   plant is positioned like any other plant, before the pond exists.
2. **Communities are not screened for water.** Two seeded communities are pond
   assemblages: "Pond & Aquatic Edge" (8 of 9 members need standing water) and
   "Open Water Submergents" (8 of 8). Either lands on dry ground as a unit.
3. **Nothing plants a pond.** The follow-up steps never add a water plant (V2.91's
   rule, written for dry yards). An animal fed only by water plants gets a note
   saying to plant one by hand where there is a pond, even when the design has
   one.

**And in the app the pond never arrived at all.** `controllers/generation.py
_render` copies only the generated *plants* into the live project. The generator
places structures, a pond, a bee hotel, a brush pile, which the AI prompt invites
("Match structures to the site: pond / swale / rain_garden…"), and the Habitat
Value Score counts them. The map never received them. No plan or test says this
was meant, and the API and MCP paths keep them. With water plants seated in the
generated pond and the pond left behind, the app would draw a ring of pond-lilies
on a lawn. So F185 includes carrying generated structures onto the map.

## The rule

**A plant that needs standing water goes in a pond, or is not placed.** Decided in
one new module, `src/pond_planting.py`, which the main pass and the follow-up
steps both ask. Every number below is a design choice and says so.

### Where in the pond

Each water plant has a role, from the body V2.90 draws it with
(`pond_habit.body_for`):

| Role | Bodies | Species | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **open water** | floating, submerged | 7: pond-lily, floating marsh-marigold, duckweed; waterweed, bladderwort, pondweed, water-milfoil | Inside the water, at most 70% of the way from the centre to the edge, so a pad does not lie half on the bank |
| **margin** | broadleaf, reed, whorled, herb, and the emergent sedges and horsetail | 16: arrowheads, water-plantain, buckbean, calla, cattail, bur-reed, bulrushes, rat root, bottle sedge, mare's-tail, water parsnip, swamp horsetail | Standing in the shallows, 85% of the way out, on the **north two-thirds of the shore** |

- **The north side** is a drawing choice with two reasons. At these latitudes the
  midday sun is in the south, so tall emergents on the north shore shade the bank
  and not the water. The south third stays open as the shallow shore the pond's
  own catalogue entry asks for ("provide a shallow shoreline for amphibians and
  birds").
- **The water is the pond's drawn water.** The ellipse is `pond_habit`'s: 0.407 ×
  0.300 of the pond's size, 4.9 × 3.6 m at the default 6 m, so a plant seated in
  the open water is the plant the 3D preview draws on it. One set of numbers, as
  V2.90's test keeps them equal to the model file.
- **Seats** fill from the middle outward in the open water (a sunflower spiral)
  and from due north outward along the shore. A seat is free when every plant
  already there is at least half the two spacings away: the same room rule the
  main pass and `open_ground` use.
- **A plant's spacing is its drawn spread**, the fields the main pass reads and
  in its order (`mature_canopy_m`, then `spacing_meters`), floored at 0.25 m
  rather than the main pass's 0.5 m, since duckweed is recorded at 0.1 m. The
  first build read the seed file's `spacing_m`, which the database calls
  `spacing_meters`, so every pond plant took the 0.5 m default and two
  pond-lilies sat 0.6 m apart.

### How many

- **Floating leaves shade at most half the water.** Each floating plant counts
  the circle of its spread (a pond-lily 1.8 m²; the default pond has 13.8 m² of
  water, so three pond-lilies). The other half stays open for the submerged
  plants' light and for whatever comes to drink.
- **Everything else is spacing.** Counted by seating one species until nothing
  fits, a 6 m pond takes 7 cattails (0.9 m spread) or 9 arrowheads (0.75 m)
  along its planted shore, 11 Sago Pondweed in its open water, and 3 pond-lilies,
  which the shade rule stops.
- **What does not fit is not placed**, and the design notes say how many did
  ("The pond had room for 2 of the 3 Cattail").
- **Water plants do not take part in density expansion.** The yard's density
  target multiplies plant groups to fill the lot; a pond has its own capacity,
  and 30 cattails is not what "balanced" means.

### Which pond

The design's own ponds first, then a pond already drawn on the site: the app
hands the generator the whole live project as `existing_features`, and a pond
there was only ever a keep-out circle.

### With no pond

The water plants are left out and the notes name them: "grow in standing water,
and this design has no pond". This is **not** the vines' option c (placed anyway,
said so). A vine without a host still grows along the ground; a pond-lily on a
lawn dies.

### A pond the spec left bare

The review plants it before the critic's repairs run, so the score and the
repairs see a planted pond:
- one floating species (one plant);
- one submerged species (two plants);
- two emergent species (two plants each).

Seven plants, from the same site-scoped pool the other follow-up steps use:
native to the yard's province, recorded near it first, then by ecological value
(keystone, larval host, pollinator, bird food: the offline generator's own
scoring). A generative rule, not a fixed community (P1). The notes name what was
planted.

**One species is never chosen automatically: *Myriophyllum sibiricum*.** The
catalogue calls it "Spiked Water-milfoil". That is the usual name of the Eurasian
*M. spicatum*, a prohibited aquatic invasive in Alberta. The row's own notes
already warn about the confusion, but a buy list carries the name, not the note.
The name needs VASCAN's English name, which a cloud session cannot fetch, so it
joins **F172**. The AI can still choose the plant by name; the rule only keeps
the generator from putting that name on a list by itself.

### Communities

A community with most of its members in water, the two pond assemblages, is
placed **into** a pond: each member seated by its role, under the community's
name. A member that does not need standing water (water smartweed in "Pond &
Aquatic Edge", which is amphibious) goes to the shore with the emergents. With no
pond the community is left out, like a single water plant.

**"Native Prairie Aromatics" is left exactly as authored.** One of its seven
members, rat root, needs standing water, and the rule would pull it out. Four of
its seven species (sweetgrass, prairie sage, rat root, Seneca snakeroot) are
widely known on the prairies as Indigenous medicines. The community's description
does not say so, and rearranging that grouping as a side effect of a placement
rule is too close to P12's line. It is flagged for the owner, not changed.

### The follow-up steps

The wildlife and goal top-ups kept a water plant out of every design (V2.91), and
said "Where you have a pond, plant it there by hand". They now **seat it in the
design's pond when there is one with room**, and keep the note when there is not.
The critic's repairs are unchanged: they take the next dry candidate, and there
always is one.

### The app

`_render` now carries each generated structure into the live project, in the same
shape the map's own structure tool writes, drawn by `map_widget.load_structure`
as a saved project's are.

**Undo needed nothing new.** `_render` already runs under `@undoable("generate
design")`: everything it adds to the project is one snapshot, and undoing it
redraws the whole map. So the design's structures undo with its plants, one step.
The first draft of this plan had a `place_structure` entry per structure, which
would have made the pond a separate undo from the plants around it.

## Left alone, on purpose

- **The pond is still placed last**, on the best cell left after the plants. Water
  plants no longer need it earlier, since they are seated after it. Siting a pond
  first (at the low point, then planting around it) is better design, but it moves
  every generated design that has a structure, so it is a separate change.
- **The offline generator places no structures**, so it never has a pond of its
  own. It can still seat water plants in a pond the user drew.
- **Depth zones.** The pond model has one water level. The margin and the open
  water are distances from the edge, not depths.
- **Rat root in "Native Prairie Aromatics"**, above.
- **Spiked Water-milfoil's name**, above (F172).

## How it is checked

`tests/test_pond_planting.py` (16 tests).

The rule, with no database:
- the 23 water plants have a role and nothing else does (7 open water, 16
  margin);
- an open-water seat is on the water `pond_habit.water_at` draws, which is what
  the 3D scene asks;
- emergents stand at the shore's reach on the north two-thirds, the first due
  north;
- seats keep their spacing;
- floating leaves never shade more than half the water, tested with leaves at the
  rim so that the shade, not the room, is what refuses the next one, while a
  submerged plant still gets a seat;
- no pond, no seat; the pond communities are the seeded assemblages;
- the milfoil is never chosen for a bare pond even when it is first in the pool.

The generator end to end, fake AI client, asked of the 3D scene:
- the spec's water plants are all in the pond, floating and submerged ones drawn
  on the water;
- with no pond they are left out, and one note names all three;
- **the dry design does not move**: the same spec with and without its water
  plants places every dry plant at the same coordinates;
- a bare pond is planted (floating, submerged, two emergents, no milfoil) and the
  notes say so;
- the "Pond & Aquatic Edge" community lands in the pond, all nine members, and
  the pond is not planted a second time;
- a pond the user drew takes the water plants and is not planted for them;
- an animal fed only by water plants gets one in the pond.

The app: `_render` carries the pond into the live project in the map tool's
shape, draws it once with `load_structure`, and still places the plants.

## Results

- **The reproduction, after**: every water plant in the pond; with no pond, one
  note naming them; a bare pond planted with Floating Marsh-marigold, Common
  Bladderwort, Buckbean and Broad-leaved Arrowhead; the pond community in the
  pond, water smartweed at the shore.
- **Neighbouring modules** (the generator, placement, critic, open ground, vine
  seating, mixes, nudges, exclusion, the guards, the placed-plant write path,
  pond habits, onboarding): 289 tests, 1 skip.
- The before-and-after sheet (the same generated design: what the V2.94 app
  showed, and V2.95) is in the session, not the repo.

- **Against V2.94**: the new test module cannot import there, since
  `src/pond_planting.py` is new, so the before is the reproduction above, run on
  V2.94's generator: 0 of 6 water plants in water, and the V2.94 sheet built from
  a V2.94 worktree.
- **Full suite** (`scripts/run_tests.py`, as CI runs it): 3,973 tests, 0 failed,
  0 errors, 7 skipped (pypdf not installed three times; four environmental),
  `RESULT: OK`.

## What could not be verified here

- **The app's map and undo** for the carried structures run in the Qt GUI, which
  this container drives only through the controller tests.
- **Against real ponds.** The shore fraction, the half-covered rule and the seven
  plants are pond-planting practice as the author knows it, not a sourced
  standard.
