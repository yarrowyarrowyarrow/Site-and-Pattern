# V3.07 — Placement, a fuller Site tab, and the quick yeses

*The owner answered every question in the V3.05 surface audit on 2 October: 37
answers, recorded on the audit's page and now in
[`SURFACE_AUDIT.md`](../SURFACE_AUDIT.md)'s Decision column. "Please begin
enacting those changes when appropriate", and, the same day: "Each time you run
the full suite it takes up a bunch of time and usage. Please bundle the coming
changes so we have at least 50% less testing along the way."*

## Three releases, one full run each

The answers come to about twenty changes. Shipped the way V3.05's leftovers
were, a release each, that is twenty full runs of the suite (about 21 minutes
each here, and again on CI). Bundled by what they touch, it is three:

| Release | What it carries |
|---|---|
| **V3.07** (this) | the answers recorded; the quick yeses (one name per window, guidance-only goals hidden, the Sprite Gallery off the menu, zoom sensitivity into Map Settings, wind with the pin); the side panel's containers: **Placement**, Site gaining Sun & Shade and Wind, Shape on the Draw row with Hedgerow folded into it, and the **Design** tab begun |
| **V3.08** | Design's merges ("What it feeds" from Bees, Wildlife and Harvest, with people's food and animals' food kept apart; "Through the years" from Timeline, This Month and Effort; Notes), the **Share** place (Present and every export, Where to buy beside the buy list), concise page instructions, one primary-button colour and dark 3D and snapshot windows, the 3D preview following edits |
| **V3.09** | the community pop-up, the synchronised map-and-satellite scan for buildings and trees, Field Study, a software-rendering fallback for 3D |

Each release runs the targeted modules while it is built and the whole suite
once, at the end, before it is pushed; CI runs it again on the push.

## The quick yeses

- **One name per window** (*q-names*). *Plant Directory* (the View menu) was
  *Field Guide* on the Learn menu, and *Walk a Reference Ecosystem…* was *Walk a
  wild landscape*. The owner said yes and gave no names, so: **Plant
  Directory**, because it says what the window is (every plant in the catalogue)
  where "field guide" promises help identifying one; and **Walk a Wild
  Landscape**, because it is plain words. The Learn menu's directory row said
  "1568 species to find", a count of plants *and* animals discovered in the
  walks, beside a window that lists 424 plants; it now says what the window
  does. The count stays on the start screen's Learn door, where it already was.
- **Guidance-only goals hidden** (*q-guidance-goals*). *Flowers all season* and
  *Visually interesting all year* carried "(guidance only — needs data)" on
  Generate Design. They are not offered now; the generator still understands
  them, so a design made with one keeps working. Worth knowing for later: the
  first of them could be backed today, since `habitat_score.parse_month_range`
  reads the catalogue's bloom months (V2.78's phenology bar uses it).
- **The Sprite Gallery is off the View menu** (*retire*), a developer's bench
  in a gardener's menu. The window stays, opened with
  `python -m src.sprite_gallery_window`.
- **Zoom sensitivity moves to View › Map Settings** (*move*). It sat at the end
  of the View row, off-screen at 1366 px. It was also never remembered: every
  launch started at Fine. In Map Settings it is saved, and applied each time the
  map loads.
- **Wind comes with the pin** (*q-wind*). The finding: the Site panel's fetch
  had read the site's wind rose with every pin since V2.13, to write one line on
  Site Info, and the Wind page never saw it: it waited for its own *Fetch*
  button and fetched the same rose again. The pin's rose now fills the Wind page
  too (its dial, strength and windbreak advice), and a design opened with a pin
  shows the rose from the local cache, without the network. *Refresh* stays, for
  the current reading.

- **Shape** joins the Draw row (*structures-shapes: move*), with **Hedgerow**
  folded into its list of types (*structures-hedgerow: merge*): a hedge, a fence,
  a living fence and a windbreak are lines, and the form shows a line's width,

- **The Design tab begins a release early.** Its full form is V3.08's, but On

  its first two pages are the **Report card** (Stats, *the best page in the app,

- **Keyboard letters** follow their tabs: P, G and S go to Placement's three
  pages, **D** to Design (A went to Analysis), T to Planning.

### How the move is made

The panels stay the owners of their pages' behaviour, signals and state; only
the containers change. Pages are taken out of one tab widget and put in
another after every panel is built (`src/side_panel_layout.py`), so nothing
about how a page works moves with it. Three things in the code addressed a page
by its position and would have broken: Analysis' Habitat and Bees lookups
(`_habitat_tab_index`, `_bee_tab_index`) now hold the page itself, and the
window's tab jumps (map events, design review, onboarding) go through
`keyboard_help.show_panel`, which opens every level a page is nested in.

## Left alone, on purpose

- **Site › Features keeps its draw and mark buttons.** The sketch put existing
  trees and buildings on the Draw row, but the owner kept Features and asked for
  it to be reworked around a synchronised scan of the map and satellite layers
  (V3.09); moving its buttons now would move them twice.
- **Where to buy** stays on Site Info until the Share place exists (V3.08), where
  it goes beside the buy list.
- **Planning** is untouched: all six of its pages are merged or kept in V3.08,
  and moving them twice is churn.

## Found on the way

- **The Shape form lives in a menu, and menus close.** Checked with real mouse
  clicks under a virtual X display: choosing a type from the form's dropdown
  keeps the menu open, and Draw closes it and lights the button. The system
  colour dialog does close it (it is a popup, as the View row's Grid colour
  already behaved), so the form opens the menu again where it was, Draw one
  click away.
- **The pointer guard could not see a removed tab.** It reads its heads (Site,
  Analysis, View…) off the live window, so once Analysis was gone, "Analysis →
  Wind" would not have been looked at at all. Retired tab names are heads now,
  and a pointer at one fails. It also missed Placement's own pages: Placement
  is the first top-level tab whose page is itself a strip.
- **A test that keeps the wind marks the design changed**, and the window's
  teardown then waited on "Exit anyway?" for ever, which hung the first test
  run with no output. The test puts the flag back.
- **The surface probe had the guard's blind spot**: its walker took a page's
  tab widgets from `findChildren`, which never returns the page itself, so its
  first V3.07 run walked Placement as one empty page. Fixed in
  `scripts/surface_inventory.py`.
- **Offline, Site Info would have stated the region's wind as the site's.** The
  Wind page falls back to the regional prevailing wind when there is no network
  and nothing cached, and labels it; the one line on Site Info now comes from the
  same rose, so it says "(a regional estimate)" too, where it used to say
  "Unavailable (offline?)".

## How it is checked

- `tests/test_shape_tool.py`: one Type list (areas, zones, lines, in that
  order); an area's payload is what the Shapes page emitted, a line's what the
  Hedgerow page emitted; a zone takes its own colours; on the Draw row, Shape
  sits between Boundary and Measure, Draw arms the map and lights the button,
  finishing, Cancel or another tool puts it out, and Draw closes the menu.
- `tests/test_map_settings.py`: Fine until changed, sent when the page loads,
  a change remembered and sent, an unknown level read as Fine, the dialog's
  levels, and no zoom control left on the View row.
- `tests/test_wind_with_the_pin.py`: the pin's rose fills the Wind page and is
  kept on the design; offline with nothing cached, the regional approximation;
  a design opened shows the cached rose and is not marked changed; nothing
  cached shows nothing; the Site panel hands the rose on; the Wind page says
  where its rose comes from.
- `tests/test_app_smoke.py`, on the real window: the five tabs and every page in
  order, moved and not rebuilt; a jump opens its page from anywhere (Site ›
  Wind, the report card's habitat link, the pin step); the Bees page still
  follows the design where it now sits; D goes to Design; Shape arms the map
  for an area and a hedge, and Cancel puts it out; the pin's wind reaches the
  Wind page; the Sprite Gallery is off the menu and the walk has its new name;
  the generator's dialog offers only backed goals. The pointer guard now also
  fails on a pointer at a removed tab.
- **The surface probe**, re-run on the restructured window at 1366 × 768 with
  every button clicked: 5 tabs and 24 pages (V3.05: 6 and 26;
  Shapes and Hedgerow left the panel for the Draw row), 151 controls on pages,
  96 buttons clicked, **0 errors**. Its screenshots show the Site tab's six
  labels whole in DejaVu Sans, the report card leading Design, and the Draw row
  with Shape between Boundary and Measure.
- **The suite**, as CI runs it: 4,450 tests (V3.06: 4,423), 0 failed, 15
  skipped, `RESULT: OK`. The regional-estimate label came after that run and was
  checked by the smoke, wind, existing-features and glossary modules (122 tests);
  CI runs the whole suite on the pushed commit.

## What could not be verified here

- **Wind arriving over the network.** This container has none, so every pin
  here got the regional approximation; the network path is the one the Site
  panel has used since V2.13, unchanged, now handed on.
- **The `hdiutil` retry.** It runs only on a macOS runner, and only matters when
  the runner is busy; the script's syntax was checked, not a failing attempt.
- **macOS and Windows drawing.** The Shape menu was driven with real clicks on
  Linux; a native menu style may size the form differently.
