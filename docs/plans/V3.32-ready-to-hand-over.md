# V3.32 · Ready to hand over

*The owner: "skip that feedback part and do the next increment." That is the
homeowner plan's increment 15 ([11-work-plan.md](../homeowner/11-work-plan.md),
part [10](../homeowner/10-finishing.md) §10), F243 and the rest of F242:
watch five people use it, then hand it over.*

**Done when** (the work plan's line): four of five homeowners complete 01 §6's
tasks 1, 2, 6 and 7 unaided; the fixes they forced are in; the friend has cut a
release; the release is promoted as the finished one. **None of that can happen
in a session**: it is five people's evenings, a collaborator the owner adds, and
a promotion the owner names. So this increment builds everything those steps
need and leaves the steps themselves to the owner, said plainly below.

The feedback address stays empty on the owner's word ("I'll set up a separate
email"); the runbook says where it goes.

## What was there

- The sessions' method, tasks and done rule, written in 01 §6, and nowhere to
  record a session.
- No runbook: cutting a release, promoting it and the yearly checks lived in
  CLAUDE.md's long notes, written for sessions, not for a person.
- The build pinned its runners (V3.31) but not its libraries: `requirements.txt`
  sets floors, so a rebuild next year would take whatever Qt was newest.

## What changed

**A walkthrough before the walkthroughs (FN-9).** `tests/test_homeowner_path.py`
does the session's seven tasks in the real window, in order, in the simple app,
through the controls the script points a person at: the address box and its
list, a bed outlined (the page's finished shape handed to Python), *Plan my yard*
in the strip with the dialog's defaults, the Overview's street item, a plant
clicked on the map and its page, Share's *Export PDF…* and *Order file…*, Save,
and the start screen's first row. It found a task that could not be done at
all: **task 5, swap a plant, had no way in the simple app.** The Overview offers
a swap only for a plant that is not native, and a plan holds natives only. So
the plant page now has **Swap…** whenever the plant is in the design, using the
Overview's dialog (OV-10) and its one undo step; the dialog says "Instead of
Wild Bergamot" for a native plant rather than calling it not native, and the
page shows the plant swapped in. The test cannot say whether a person finds a
control; it says every task has a way through, which is what a session should be
able to assume.

**Session notes (FN-9).** A page, published privately to the owner and meant to
be shared with the friend as an editor
(<https://claude.ai/artifact/1zUREu4ApaMbTfBLTrJN4D>, source
`tools/usability/sessions.html`, README beside it): the setup checklist, the
seven tasks worded to be read aloud with what counts as done, one sheet per
person (result, minutes, where they stopped and why, words they did not know,
what they expected), whether four of five passed tasks 1, 2, 6 and 7, every task
that stopped someone side by side so a repeat shows, and the unknown words
counted for the glossary. It keeps its rows in its own database, so a later
session reads them back (`ArtifactData list` on `sessions`) to write the results
into a plan and the words into 09-words.md. It holds no names or addresses and
says so. It was published empty: inventing example people in a shared record
would be worse than an empty sheet.

**The runbook (FN-13, FN-14).** `docs/MAINTENANCE.md`, short: what is being
looked after; cutting a release in four steps (V-branch, three green jobs, the
prerelease tried on a real laptop with the example, a plan and a PDF, promote on
the release page); the tests; the pinned build; the yearly checks (the five
nurseries, the services' terms with Esri's still unread, the domain); a table of
what the app does when each service breaks, checked against the code's own
messages; feedback; what not to do; and the access checklist only the owner can
tick (collaborator, one release cut by the maintainer, the notes page shared,
where the registrar and mailbox are held). The README's status line says
"nearly finished" and points to both.

**The libraries pinned (FN-12).** `scripts/packaging/constraints-windows.txt`
and `constraints-macos.txt` carry the exact versions from V3.31's green builds'
`build-versions.txt` (read from the two build logs: PyQt6 6.11.0 with Qt 6.11.2
on Windows, 6.7.1 with Qt 6.7.3 on the Mac for Big Sur). The Windows script and
the macOS workflow install against them. Part 10 §9 asked for the versions of
*the release that passed the sessions*; the sessions have not run, so these are
the last good build's, and moving one is a line, a push and a try.
