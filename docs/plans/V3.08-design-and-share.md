## The shape

Five tabs: **Site · Placement · Design · Share · Learn**. Planning is gone:
every one of its six pages was merged or moved.

| Tab | Pages |
|---|---|
| Site | Site Info · Slope · Sun & Shade · Wind · Features · **Notes** |
| Placement | Plants · Communities · Structures |
| Design | Report card · Planted · Habitat · **Food** · **Over time** · Water |
| Share | **Present** · **Export** |
| Learn | Field Study · Lessons |

- **Food** is the owner's "what it feeds" page (`src/food_page.py`, its
  arithmetic in `src/what_it_feeds.py`), named short so six labels fit the
  strip whole: *What it feeds* and *Through the years* together would have
  elided at 1366 px. It holds both halves of the answer.
  - **One animal, any animal.** The Bees page answered "design for this bee"
    for 120-odd bees and genera. The same question now takes any of the
    catalogue's animals: which of the design's plants feed it and how (nectar,
    pollen, larval host, fruit, seed, nest site, cover), which plants in the
    catalogue would, and the months it finds food here. A bee keeps everything
    the Bees page said (tongue fit, nesting, its flight season). The list opens
    on the animal the design feeds most and keeps a person's own choice while
    the design changes; a find box narrows its 700-odd rows.
  - **Month by month, people kept apart.** The Wildlife and Harvest calendars
    were two trees over the same months. They are one now, and each month
    holds three groups in a fixed order, *for pollinators*, *for birds* and
    **for people**, the last in its own colour, so a harvest is never read as
    forage. A plant with nothing edible recorded is never a harvest.
- **Over time** is the owner's *Through the years*: **This month** (the
  phenology dashboard), **Year by year** (the succession slider and the phased
  conversion plan), **Hours of work** (the effort estimate). One scrolling page,
  the near question first.
- **Notes** on Site is Field Notes with the **design journal** and the notes
  pinned on the map under the site walk: one place to write things down.
- **Planted** says, under each species, what eats it and when: "feeds 137: 53
  bees, 35 butterflies and moths, 13 birds, 36 other insects · flowers May–Jun ·
  fruit Aug–Sep". Under each community, the same for its members together, each
  animal counted once.
- **Share** holds what leaves the app: the narrated tour (**Present**), and
  **Export**: the PDF, the planting plan, the order file, Growth Snapshots, the
  3D preview's presentation still and before / after, and **Where to buy**,
  beside the buy list it serves. Every button there calls what the File menu or
  the 3D preview already does; nothing is a second way of exporting.
- The report card's **Estimated cost ›** link opened the Planning tab, which had
  no page about cost; it opens Share › Export, where the buy list is.

Pages are moved as V3.07 moved them (`src/side_panel_layout.py`); the merged
pages are new containers around the old pages' working parts, except Food,
whose bee code moved out of `analysis_panel.py`, generalised.

## The 3D preview, colours, words

- **The 3D preview follows the design** (F89, `src/follow_design.py`). Every
  edit ends in `_mark_modified`, the hook split view has used since V2.44, and
  a design opened or undone ends in `_sync_planning_panel`; both ask the open
  preview to rebuild once the edits pause (400 ms, so a drag is one rebuild).
  *Refresh from design* is gone. **Growth Snapshots had the same manual
  Refresh** and follows by the same rule. A closed window pays nothing.
- **One colour for the main action** (F209). The audit counted six primaries in
  five colours; three were left that were not green: *Show sun path* (orange),
  *Refresh wind data* (teal) and *Show Wind Overlay* (blue). (The audit's purple
  is in no button stylesheet of V3.06 or later; it went before this release.)
  The two Shows are the app's green; *Refresh wind data* became grey, because
  since V3.07 the rose arrives with the pin and Refresh is the supporting
  action. A test reads every button stylesheet in `src/` and fails on a
  saturated fill that is not green.
- **Dark windows** (F209). The 3D preview and Growth Snapshots are top-level
  windows, so they inherited none of the main window's stylesheet and opened in
  the platform's light palette. So was **Walk a Wild Landscape**, which the
  answer did not name and the probe's screenshot did: its pale-green
  description, labels and hint sat on light grey, nearly invisible. The app's
  stylesheet moved from `app.py` to `ui_style.APP_STYLE`, and all three
  windows wear `ui_style.WINDOW_STYLE`, which adds buttons that show when they
  are on (the preview's View row is checkable). The Plant Directory was dark
  already.
- **Concise pages** (F210). Nineteen opening paragraphs cut to what the page
  cannot say by itself, 3,487 characters down to 2,103 (two fifths): Site
  Info, Slope and its download note, Sun & Shade, Features (both), Notes and
  its photo note, Habitat (four), This month, Year by year, Hours of work,
  Water, Present, Where to buy and Field Study. What went: lists the page shows anyway (the Habitat
  score's parts), restatements of the heading, advice ("Great prep for a
  nursery or trail visit"), and slogans ("A landscape is a trajectory, not an
  install day"). What stayed: anything a person needs to act (Generate needs an
  area; a finer grid only interpolates the 30 m elevation; Where to buy's
  source and "check before you go").

## Found on the way

- **"Caterpillar host" was wrong for a bee or a beetle.** The edges layer's
  `larval_host` covers butterflies and moths, and also bees (GloBI's *hostOf*,
  from a specimen's label: the flowers it was taken on) and other insects whose
  larvae eat the plant. The first cut of Food called them all caterpillar
  hosts, and gave a bee's host the months a leaf is out. `what_it_feeds.
  kind_words` and `months_for` now take the animal's group: a caterpillar host,
  a bee's host plant (its months are the bloom's), a larval host for the rest.
- **The Slope download note said SRTM.** The elevation outside Edmonton is the
  Copernicus 30 m model through Open-Meteo (`src/terrain.py`); the note no
  longer names a model.
- **The 3D preview's edit hint was invisible.** It is `#cfe8d2`, written for a
  dark window, and the window was light: about 1.2:1. The dark window is what
  it was written for.
- **A strip hides the page it gives up.** Pages moved into another strip are
  shown by that strip, but the three that went into a layout (Year by year,
  Hours of work, the journal) stayed hidden: the first build drew *Year by
  year* and *Hours of work* as headings over nothing, and Notes without its
  journal. Every test passed, because they asked where the pages were, not
  whether they could be seen; **the surface probe's screenshots** showed it.
  The pages are shown now, and the tests ask `isVisible()`.
- **A moved page stays its old stack's child.** `QStackedWidget` keeps the
  parent of a widget it removes until that widget is deleted, so the emptied
  This Month page and Planning's empty strip were still in the window's tree,
  unnamed, and the accessibility guard found both. They are taken out of the
  window when they are emptied (`side_panel_layout._discard`).
- **The Planted rows ran off the list.** Qt measured each wrapped row without
  the list's item padding, so rows were 12 to 30 px wider than the list: a
  sideways scroll bar, and the end of each line cut off. A small delegate
  (`_WrapToList`) sizes each row to the list.
- **The hours table hid most of the plants.** It listed six fixed types (tree,
  shrub, herb, groundcover, vine, root), so wildflowers (198 of the 424
  plants), grasses, sedges and rushes went into the subtotal with no row: the
  worked example showed 17 hours of rows over a 55-hour subtotal. Every type in
  the design has its row now, in the catalogue's order (`member_colors`).
- **A refill could not follow a page to a new strip.** `LiveRefresh` connected
  its tab widget's `currentChanged` to a lambda, which cannot be disconnected
  by name; `move_to` re-targets it, so Effort and Water fill themselves in
  Design as they did in Planning.
- **The address search covers Alberta only** (`site_panel`'s Nominatim query is
  bounded to it), while the generator (V2.85) and the plant list's Native filter
  (F200, V3.05) follow the pin into Saskatchewan. Not changed here: numbered
  **F212**.

## Left alone, on purpose

- **F86's one store.** Notes is one *page*; the stores (field notes, map notes,
  the journal, photo notes) stay separate. What a note should be able to *do*
  is still the owner's question.
- **Habitat stays its own page**, after the report card, as the owner kept it.
- **Keyboard.** T went to Planning, which is gone; no letter is invented for
  Share.
- **The 3D preview's creature list** (721 rows no typing can search, F196's
  other copy). Moving the window's two workers out (`src/scene3d_workers.py`)
  took it from 950 lines, its ceiling, to 903; the list is V3.09's or later.
- **The Planning panel object.** Its pages all moved, but it still owns their
  state, signals and calculations, so it stays, hidden. Dissolving it into the
  pages' new homes is churn with no one asking for it.

## How it is checked

- `tests/test_what_it_feeds.py` (12): months read as runs, groups in plain
  words, a planted species' line, "no animal recorded" for nothing recorded;
  when an animal eats is read off the plant, a larval host worded and timed by
  the animal's group; against the real catalogue, every animal fed listed once
  and most plants first, a community counting each animal once, one animal
  against the design (its plants, others that would serve it, food first, its
  gap months), an animal the design does not feed as a shopping list, and
  people kept apart (nothing without an edible part is a harvest, no grass is a
  pollinator's bloom).
- `tests/test_food_page.py` (12): opens on the animal the design feeds most;
  any animal, not only bees; a bee keeps the Bees page's sections; a choice kept
  while the design changes; the list reaching beyond the design; the find box;
  each month's three groups with people last and Chokecherry's fruit a harvest;
  an empty design; the map overlay; Planted's species and community lines, and
  its rows wrapping inside the list (12 px of sideways scroll without the fix).
- `tests/test_share_panel.py` (5): the exports in order with what each gives;
  the documents call the File menu's handlers and the pictures open the window
  that renders them; Where to buy lands above the stretch.
- `tests/test_follow_design.py` (10): a burst of edits is one rebuild of each open
  window; a closed one pays nothing; an edit made in the preview is not rebuilt
  twice; a failing rebuild does not end the app; both hooks call it; no button
  filled in a colour of its own; Snapshots dark with no Refresh; every window of
  its own wears the dark style; the window style is the app's own.
- `tests/test_live_pages.py`: Food fills itself on screen; a live page moved
  to another strip is still filled while the strip it left no longer drives it;
  every type in the design has its row in the hours.
- `tests/test_forage_calendar.py`: no grass among the pollinators' blooms, read
  off Food's month by month now.
- `tests/test_scene3d_window.py`: no Refresh, dark, and `follow_design` rebuilds
  the scene with the design's new tree.
- `tests/test_app_smoke.py`, on the real window: the five tabs and every page in
  order, moved and not rebuilt; Over time's three sections in order and shown;
  the hours and water fill themselves in Design; Notes holds the walk, then the
  journal, then the map's notes, all shown; Share holds Present, the exports and
  Where to buy; the cost link opens Export; Food follows the design where it
  sits and is not worked out out of sight; the pointer guard now fails on
  "Planning" too.
- **The surface probe**, at 1366 × 768 with every button clicked, twice: the
  first run found the hidden sections above (0 errors, which is why the
  screenshots matter); after the fix, 5 tabs and 26 pages, 153 controls on
  pages, 98 buttons clicked, **0 errors**. The screenshots show Design's six
  labels whole, Over time with its three sections, Notes with the journal, the
  Export page with Where to buy, and the 3D preview and Growth Snapshots dark.
  Walk a Wild Landscape was darkened after that run and photographed on its
  own: its description, labels and hint read, and Walk shows as on.
- **The suite**, as CI runs it, once: 4,498 tests (V3.07: 4,450), 0 failed, 0
  errors, 15 skipped, `RESULT: OK`. CI runs it again on the pushed commit.

## What could not be verified here

- **A real screen reader** over the new pages; the accessibility guard checks
  names, not speech.
- **The 3D preview rebuilding in front of a person.** The follow is driven and
  timed in tests with stand-in windows, and the real window's rebuild with a
  stubbed page; this container's WebGL is software, so how long a rebuild of a
  large design takes on a laptop is not known.
- **macOS and Windows drawing** of the dark windows' controls; Fusion on Linux
  is what was looked at.
