# V3.44 · Getting to the 3D preview, and a window a homeowner can use (3D-2, F261)

*The owner, 12 November 2026: "Continue on the next increment of the 3D work on a new branch
V3.44, the recommendations are fine with me."*

The second of the eight increments in
[the V3.43 review](V3.43-the-3d-preview-reviewed.md), which is the handover and holds the
findings, the guardrails and the decisions. This file is what 3D-2 found, decided and built.

## The decisions this increment needed

Both answered by "the recommendations are fine with me":

| | Decision | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | The door: open 3D from the simple app without switching Advanced tools on, or M2 as written | **Without switching.** The door opens the window and leaves the mode alone (U21 of the V3.37 use test found that switch disorienting) |

## What was measured before building

On the review lot (15.2 x 36.6 m, 367 placed plants), with the code as V3.43 left it:

- **Every scene push asked the internet for the wind.** `build_scene` calls
  `wind_scene._rose_for`, which called `wind.get_wind_summary`, which on a cache miss downloads
  two years of hourly wind from Open-Meteo with a 20 second timeout. On the app's main thread, on
  every slider tick, for as long as the cache is empty: anyone offline, and anyone whose pin's
  own fetch failed. The comment at `build_scene` says wind "can touch the DB cache"; it could
  touch the network. The suite never saw it because the suite's network guard fails the request
  at once. In the profile it was 0.23 s of every push, spent on a refused connection.
- **The shade simulation ran from nothing on every push.** `SuccessionEngine` keeps what it has
  worked out per plant and year, and `build_scene` made a new engine each time. Year 0: 0.24 s;
  year 5: 0.47 s; year 10: 0.54 s; year 25: 0.86 s (2.1 of 2.5 s under the profiler is
  `evaluate_year`). Moving from year 24 to 25 cost the same as starting at 25.
- **A drag of the Year slider across its range is up to 25 pushes**, each a full rebuild in Python
  and in the page.
- The window's first creature is *Agapostemon femoratus*: the first row of a list sorted by genus,
  Latin-only, whether or not this design holds a plant it uses.
- The card's text is 9 to 11.5 px in thirteen rules; the visual floor test (12 px) did not read
  `html/scene3d.html` or the viewer's scripts.

## What V3.44 built

**The door (M2, D1).** *See it in 3D* on the plan strip (`plan_bar.py`) and as the link on the
Overview's *How it will look from the street* (`yard_overview_items.SEE_3D`, target
`view3d:street`, handled by `yard_overview_flow`), whatever that item's status. Neither touches
the Advanced tools switch. From the street item it shows the street view even when the window is
already open on another; from the plan strip it opens, or raises, and leaves the camera alone.
`test_homeowner_path` presses the Overview's link in task 3 and reads the window it opens.

**Two short rows (finding 13).** Every control named:

- *When*: First summer · Third summer · Fifth summer · Tenth summer · Twentieth summer · Fully
  grown.
- *Season*: Spring · Summer · Fall · Winter (May, July, September, January).
- *Time of day*: Morning · Midday · Afternoon · Evening · Night (9, 12, 15, 19, 23 h).
- *View*: From the street · From the house · From above · Walk · Reset view.

The names and their values are one Qt-free table, `src/scene3d_stops.py`; the widgets are two new
groups of `scene3d_toolbar.Scene3DToolBar` (`WHEN`, `VIEWS`, built by
`src/scene3d_named_groups.py`), so the walk window can take them later without a second
implementation. The window's sliders stay the one record of the moment (a dozen places read them,
and the before/after page and the stills set them); the lists set the sliders and follow them.
**A list never says a stop it is not at:** with the fine sliders on year 7, *When* reads
"Seventh summer", an entry that exists only while it is true.

With **Advanced tools** on, the rows the window had come back under those two: the fine sliders,
Detail, Flyover, Identify, the pollinator tools, Plant and Remove, the two outputs. They are one
container (`Scene3DWindow.advanced_rows`), registered in `advanced_tools.py` as a hook
(`scene3d_tools`, a holder the main window always has, since the 3D window does not exist until
it is opened and the registry's test wants every entry present). Turning the switch off while one
of those tools is running stops it, as it cancels Measure on the map.

**What opens (D5).** The fifth summer, July, 15:00, from the street, for each design the window is
shown: the opening view is sent after the first scene of a design, and again when another design
is opened, and never while Walk, a flight or the Flyover owns the camera. **From the street only
when the planting can be seen from it** (`scene_views.opening_view`): a back-yard plan seen from
the street is the front of the house, so where a building stands in the way from every spot
tried, or all of the planting is in the half of the yard away from the street, the window opens
from the house, and with no house on the whole yard. An empty design opens on the whole stage, as
it always did.

**From the house** (`scene_views.house_view`, `views.house`). The house is the largest building
standing in the yard's outline; with nothing outlined, the building the yard's pin stands on. The
person stands a step outside the wall most of the planting is nearest to, at the spot on that wall
closest to that planting, moved along it until no trunk or other building is in the way and
nothing that grows tall is within arm's reach, and looks across at the planting. Like the street
view it is worked out from the design as planted, so it is the same spot in every year. **With no
such building there is no such view, and the button says what it needs** rather than guessing
where a house might be (P9). *From above* is straight down, north at the top, fitted to the
design.

The three are drawn by a new chunk, `html/scene3d/27-views.js` (`window.permaShowView`):
`08-modes.js` has eleven lines left.

**A yard with no outline is a yard.** The simple app's usual design is a pin and a bed, with no
outline (V3.28: "a bed with no outline is enough"), and such a design had no `views` at all: the
street camera fell back to V3.42's guess south of the stage and the walker started at the centre
of the stage. Its own extent now stands in for the outline, and the person's answer about which
side the street is on still moves it. A walk through a wild community is not a yard and keeps
none.

**Sliders that keep up (finding 14).**

- Pushes are coalesced: the first move pushes at once, and after that at most one every 80 ms,
  of whatever the sliders say by then. The labels move at once.
- The shade simulation is kept for the design (`build_scene(..., memo=)`, a dict the window
  owns): the engine is reused while the placed plants, the shade casters, the origin and the
  catalogue are what they were. The scenes are identical with and without it.
- The scene reads the wind from the cache only (`wind_scene._cached_rose`). The pin's own fetch
  (off the main thread, since V1.67) is what fills it.

On the review lot, a scene with the memo against one without, same machine, same run:

| year, in the order visited | 5 | 0 | 1 | 10 | 25 | 24 | 5 again | 25 again |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V3.43's path, with the wind fixed (s) | 0.27 | 0.04 | 0.09 | 0.44 | 0.75 | 0.77 | 0.18 | 0.52 |
| with the simulation kept (s) | 0.30 | 0.01 | 0.01 | 0.19 | 0.32 | 0.09 | 0.01 | 0.02 |

(The wind's refused connection was another 0.23 s on every one of the top row before it was
fixed. The suite was running beside this, so the figures are comparable with each other, not
with a quiet machine.)

**The simple app's rules in the window.**

- Card text at 12 px or more, and the floor test now reads `html/scene3d.html` and
  `html/scene3d/*.js`.
- The Creatures speed bar shows only while animals are the subject: walking, flying as one,
  Identify, *Show its plants*, or an animal's card open. It sat over every picture. It also stays
  for as long as the animals are slowed or paused, and Space obeys the same rule, because a scene
  frozen with nothing on screen saying so reads as a hang.
- The pollinator the tools start on has an English name and is the one the records have feeding
  on the most plants standing in this design (`scene3d_creatures.default_row`); with none, the
  first with an English name. The person's own choice is never replaced.
- The line at the top of the picture says the year in the toolbar's words ("Fifth summer"; "Fifth
  year" in the months a yard is not in its summer), from a copy a test holds equal to the table.
- The window's modules join the vocabulary ratchet's simple list (one tooltip said *species*).
- The buttons the page draws itself (a card's close and back, *Plant one*, the speed steps) have
  a 24 px floor, which the app-wide target-size filter cannot reach inside a web page.

## Corrections made while building

- **One row became two.** The plan said one row. In CI's font it is 1,095 px wide and the window
  opens at 960, so the moment and the view are a row each; the window's least width is 613 px.
- **A yard with no outline** (above) was not in the plan. The homeowner-path test found it: its
  design is a pin and a bed, and the picture it opened had no street view to open on.
- **The view from the house first stood in the foundation bed.** The first contact sheet showed
  grasses against the lens. The rule gained "nothing that grows tall within arm's reach", by
  grown height so it does not move from year to year; on the review lot the person now stands
  between the path and the driveway.
- **Which wall** is decided by how many plants are nearest it, which a test of mine got wrong: the
  gate's yard has thirty forbs behind the house and eleven plants in front, so its view is from
  the back wall.
- **The speed bar** was first hidden whenever animals were not the subject, which would have left
  a paused yard with no way to see why it had stopped. It stays while they are slowed or paused.
- **A back-yard plan would have opened on the front of its house.** Found by thinking through
  D5 on the second kind of plan Plan My Yard makes, not by a test. The street view now says when
  the planting is behind (`views.street.behind`) and the window opens elsewhere.
- **The Plant tool's year.** The year on the slider is the year a plant goes in (V2.44). With the
  window opening on the fifth summer, a plant put in from 3D is five years behind the rest, where
  on V3.43's opening (year 0) it aged with the design. The rule is unchanged; the hint beside the
  tool now says it ("Planting Wild Bergamot in the fifth summer.") and follows the slider.
- **Every year has words**, not only the stops: "Seventh summer", not "Year 7", so the line over
  the picture does not change its form as the slider crosses a stop.
- **Line ceilings.** The toolbar's two groups took it past its ceiling, so they are a mixin of
  their own; the window went past its own, so the Flyover's storyboard moved to the stops table
  and the pollinator list to `scene3d_creatures.py`. The window is at 932 of 950.
- **The baseline was run twice.** The first run was on the working tree while I edited it, and
  tests that import inside a test function were reading the new code. It was thrown away and run
  again in a clean checkout of V3.43.

## What is left alone

- **The walk window's toolbar** keeps its sliders. It can take `WHEN` in one line; whether a walk
  through a wild landscape wants "Fifth summer" is a question about that window, not this one.
- **The View menu's action** stays with the Advanced tools (M2).
- **The pollinator list** still cannot be searched by typing (what is left of F89). It is an
  advanced tool now, and starts on an animal the design feeds.
- **A yard outlined without its house** has no view from it, and neither has a pin set down on the
  lawn. The button says so. Guessing the nearest building would hand some people their
  neighbour's wall.
- **Split view** (advanced) keeps its own pane and has no toolbar.
- The picture itself: beds, paths, the street, roofs (3D-3), seasons and the shade deaths said out
  loud (3D-4), light (3D-6). Opening on the fifth summer shows the review lot with a third of its
  plants lost to shade, which the line at the top counts; 3D-4 and 3D-5 are what answer it.

## Not verified

- **A real graphics card, Windows, macOS, and frame rate.** The gate and the sheets are headless
  Chromium drawing in software; the app's own QtWebEngine was driven offscreen with its page's
  calls captured, not drawn. The readout (Ctrl+Shift+F) is there for the owner's laptop.
- **How the two rows look in Windows' and the Mac's fonts.** They were measured and looked at in
  DejaVu Sans, CI's font and the widest of the three.
- **How a drag feels.** One push per 80 ms is tested as a rule; nobody has dragged the slider on a
  real desktop since.
- **The wind in the picture for someone online.** The scene reads what the pin's fetch cached;
  that fetch could not run here (no session reaches Open-Meteo), so the two were tested apart.
