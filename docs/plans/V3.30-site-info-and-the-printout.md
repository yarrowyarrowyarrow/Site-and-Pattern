## What was built

**Site Info in four lines** (WD-2, `src/site_summary.py`, Qt-free). Under the
address box:

> **Where:** 10423 81 Avenue NW, Strathcona, in the Edmonton region.
> **Cold:** Plants hardy to zone 3b grow here. Frost-free from about May 15 to
> September 16 (typical for the Edmonton region).
> **Rain:** About 460 mm a year, most of it in June and July.
> **Soil:** Loam, slightly alkaline (pH 7.4), typical for the Edmonton region.

Each line says when its figure is not the yard's own (*typical for the
Edmonton region*: a station normal, the pack's frost dates, the regional soil
atlas), and **no line is shown for a value the app does not have**: a pin in
Toronto gets only *Where*. *Details ▸* opens everything the page showed
before (growing-degree days, snow, soil make-up, every source, the
coordinates); the Advanced tools switch opens it by default. The soil
download and the communities link are advanced-only (`ADVANCED_CONTROLS`),
each in a box of its own so the fetch can still show and hide the link
inside. The *Edmonton region.* line under the address now shows only for a
pin outside the region, where it says what that changes: inside, *Where*
says it.

## Deliberately left

- **Other surfaces' pin words** (*Drop a property pin…* on Wind, Slope and
  the soil-prep sheet): advanced pages, for WD-6's wider pass.
- **The rain line rounds to 10 mm** and names the two wettest months; a
  year whose wettest months are not adjacent reads *June and August*.
- **The frost line for a pin outside every pack** in the provinces uses the
  nearest pack's station normal (ED-6), and says *typical for the Edmonton
  region*, which is what the figure is.
