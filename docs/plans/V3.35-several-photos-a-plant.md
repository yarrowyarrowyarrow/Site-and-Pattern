# V3.35 · Several photographs a plant

*The owner, after the colour review: "I greatly enjoyed the multiple photos i
got to see of each plant. It would be nice to add more photos (with appropriate
licensing for sharing in my app/website)." On the V3.34 plan's three open
decisions: "go with your recommendations".*

F251, with F247 (the top 60's photographs) riding on it. Planned in
[V3.34](V3.34-colours-in-photos-next.md), part 2, where the measurement that
shaped it is recorded.

## The decisions, answered

| | Decision | What it means here |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Keep the bar: CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA | `photo_picks.LICENCES`, held equal to the data gate's set by a test. V3.34 measured it: every plant iNaturalist knows has five or more observations whose photo may ship |
| D2 | **The flower leads**, the whole plant second | `PHOTO_SLOTS` now starts `flower, habit` (it led with `habit` from V2.35), and the photo the owner stars leads whatever its slot |
| D3 | **The page suggests, the owner confirms or swaps** | `photo_picks.prepick`: the most-liked photo in flower first, then one each of the whole plant, the fruit and winter |
| D4 | No photo that may ship: say so and keep a list | The page says so on the plant's card; the app and the website keep saying "No photo yet" |

## What was built

1. **`src/photo_picks.py`**, the rule every other part reads: the licences,
   the kinds of photo (`GROUPS`, each filed under a slot), the slot words
   ("in fruit"), the suggestion, `validate` (a plant in the catalogue, at most
   six, each one of iNaturalist's open photographs with a licence that may ship
   and a credit), `apply` (the picks replace a plant's rows in
   `data/plant_photos.json`, ranked in the owner's order, and the first becomes
   the one photo in `plants_master.json`), and the credit link: a photo's page
   on iNaturalist, derived from its URL, so no column was added.
2. **`scripts/fetch_photo_candidates.py`**, on the owner's PC. Per plant:
   research-grade observations filtered by `photo_license` (and every photo's
   licence checked again), favourites first, Alberta and Saskatchewan before
   anywhere, in four kinds from iNaturalist's Plant Phenology annotation
   (flowering 13, fruiting 14, no evidence of flowering 21) and the winter
   months for trees, shrubs and vines; plus the photos on the species' own
   iNaturalist page and the photo it has now. At most four of each kind, none
   under 500 px, two at most from one photographer. The taxon ids come from
   V3.34's count, so most plants cost no lookup; MacKenzie's Hedysarum, which
   iNaturalist does not know by the catalogue's name, is tried under the name
   VASCAN accepts (`data_quality.KNOWN_NOMENCLATURE`). The order of the list:
   no photo first, then best picks first, ranked on a throwaway copy of the
   catalogue (three seconds; the owner's own data is not touched).
3. **The picking page**, written by the same script, in the shape of the
   colour review: one card per plant, the suggestion as *Your picks*, every
   candidate in one grid labelled with its kind, "elsewhere" when it was taken
   outside the two provinces, and its likes. Click to add or remove, *Lead* to
   move one to the front, a slot per pick, *Use these* or *Keep the photo it
   has*. Work stays in the browser; *Save my picks* writes `photo_picks.json`.
   The first draft gave each kind its own row and a plant took 1,300 px; one
   grid takes about 880.
4. **`scripts/fold_photo_picks.py`**, in a session: checks every pick and
   writes nothing if one fails, then folds them in. A schema bump follows.
5. **The app** (`src/photo_strip.py`): the plant page shows the selected photo
   large, its credit linked to its iNaturalist page, and the others as 48 px
   thumbnails, each named for a screen reader ("Photo 2 of 4: the whole
   plant") with its credit. The strip fetches its own thumbnails off the UI
   thread, and the page keeps the one chosen across a redraw.
   `species_page.py` lost 33 lines to it, 17 under its ceiling before.
6. **The website** (`src/static_site_gallery.py`): the lead as before, then the
   rest as thumbnails linked to their larger copies, and a numbered credit for
   each, linked. The build stages every credited photo, not only the first.
   Split out because `static_site_render.py` was 3 lines and
   `static_site_species.py` 7 under their ceilings.
7. **The data gate checks the licence of every row in `plant_photos.json`.**
   *Corrected from the V3.34 plan*, which said the gate never read the file: it
   checked each row's slot, URL and credit, but not its licence, which only
   `plants_master.json`'s one photo had checked.

## The order of a plant's photographs

`src.db.plants.photo_order` is the one key, used by the list every screen
shows (`_attach_photos`) and by `photos.photos_for`: a person's own photograph
first, then the owner's picked order (`rank`), then the slot (the flower
first), then age. Before, the slot came first, so a picked order could not
win across slots, and `_attach_photos` handed out its list in `rank` order
while choosing its one photo by slot: the list and the photo could disagree.
On today's data nothing visible changes before any picks arrive: every rank
is 0, and the one plant with a whole-plant shot (Ascending Milkvetch) has no
other photo to lead with.

## How it was checked

- The rules, the fetch (iNaturalist replaced), the fold and the gallery by
  `tests/test_photo_picks.py`; the strip and the page by
  `tests/test_photo_strip.py`, on real widgets with local images.
- **The picking page in headless Chromium**: the test presses its own buttons
  (confirm a suggestion, take a photo out and put it back, *Lead*, *Keep*) and
  folds what the page saves through `photo_picks.validate`. Its first draft
  pressed *Lead* on a card where it had just removed the second pick, so the
  button was not there; a page that runs and never reports now fails the
  test rather than skipping it.
- A dry run of the whole script with a fake iNaturalist: ranking, the cache,
  the page; and screenshots of the page and of the app's plant page.

## Could not verify

- **iNaturalist's answers**, from here. Fruiting (14) and no flowers (21), the
  `month` filter, `original_dimensions` and the taxon page's `taxon_photos`
  have never been seen; `--probe` prints each and stops on a field that is not
  there. If `original_dimensions` is missing the size floor is skipped rather
  than every photo refused.
- Whether every photo that may ship is on the open-data store: the 311 in the
  catalogue are, and a candidate that is not is left out (the probe shows the
  URLs).
- The strip on a real desktop with real photographs and a screen reader.

## Left alone

- **Animals' photographs**: the bees' stricter bar (CC0, CC BY) is its own
  decision; the same tools can do animals later.
- **A lightbox on the website**: a thumbnail links to the larger copy, no
  script, as the rest of the site.
- **The bench** (`scripts/tune_morphology.py`) keeps its own photo sorting;
  its slot list follows D2's order, which a test holds.
