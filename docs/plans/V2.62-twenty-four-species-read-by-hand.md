## F128 — the citations, applied

The observation fetch returned **13,665 edges with a citation on every one**.
Not the outcome the single-plant probe predicted: it had suggested 6% life
stages, and the full corpus gave **41%**. The probe sample was one plant and
not representative — worth remembering next time a probe reports a
percentage.

Body part stayed at **0%** across all 13,665, confirming the negative result
rather than softening it. The sapsucker and mammal-browse records are not
recoverable from `/interaction` in any mode.

### What GloBI calls a "study"

Four different things, and each needed its own handling before the
bibliography would accept them:

| shape | count | what it becomes |
|---|---|---|
| a real paper (`Glazener 1946`) | 119 distinct | `kind: study`, authors split on the first year |
| a portal URL (`https://ecdysis.org/…?occid=…`) | 1,357 distinct, **8 hosts** | `kind: specimen_record`, one entry per collection |
| a GBIF dataset UUID | 10 | `kind: dataset`, resolvable at gbif.org |
| `urn:catalog:USDA-ARS:BBSL:1176785` | 9 | `kind: specimen_record`, one entry per collection |

**Collapsing is the whole trick.** 1,357 distinct specimen URLs are 8
institutions; 9 URNs are one bee collection. Keyed per record, the
bibliography would have grown an entry per pinned beetle and stopped being a
bibliography.

Two source-data defects had to be handled rather than assumed away:

- One "citation" is `http://iNaturalist.org is a place where you can record
  what you see in nature, …` — a URL prefix glued to a prose blurb. Taking
  everything up to the next slash made the whole sentence the hostname, and
  then the bibliography key. A hostname has no spaces; only the first token
  counts now.
- A URL with no readable host falls back to citing the aggregator, because a
  key invented from nothing is worse provenance than an honest `globi`.

### The gate already knew this was coming

`data_quality` exempts `NOT_A_WORK` from needing authors, with a comment
saying that requiring one *"would force somebody to invent one, which is the
failure this whole file exists to prevent."* `specimen_record` and `dataset`
join it for exactly that reason, from the other side: **a pinned beetle in the
USDA-ARS collection has a collector, a locality and a date, and no author.**

## The introduced review — 24 species, read

V2.61 tried twice to make GBIF answer "is this introduced to Canada" and
failed twice:

1. **The occurrence facet** returns nothing at all, on any species, including
   a starling with 267,995 Alberta records.
2. **The checklist distributions** return values, and **contradict
   themselves**. Filtering strictly to Canada rows fixed the Summer Tanager
   and Ruffed Grouse, made the Alder Flycatcher and Red-eyed Vireo *worse* —
   `conflicted` became a flat `introduced` — and lost *Apis mellifera*, the
   most obviously introduced insect in the country.

No filter fixes a source that disagrees with itself. **But the shortlist was
24 species out of 3,064**, and 24 is a number a person can read.

`scripts/curate_introduced.py` is that reading: **19 introduced, 5 native, 1
`unsure`**, with a reason on every line, the same shape as the bird curation.

Two findings worth keeping:

- **GBIF was right 19 times out of 24.** The adelgids, Cabbage White,
  Seven-spot, Asian Lady Beetle, European Corn Borer, Cinnabar Moth, Large
  Yellow Underwing, Brown Marmorated Stink Bug, European Skipper — all
  genuinely introduced. The signal is good; only the verdict was premature.
- **Every clear false positive is a bird.** Snow Goose, Alder Flycatcher,
  Red-eyed Vireo, Scarlet Tanager, plus one native wasp. Birds already carry
  a human verdict from V2.60, which is why none of this reached the seed data.

`unsure` exists and is used once, on an agromyzid leafminer recorded on both
sides of the Atlantic. It **fails closed** — refused, like an introduction —
because a catalogue whose whole claim is that its species belong here should
not admit one nobody can place. That is my uncertainty recorded as
uncertainty, not a finding.

### The reviewed verdict beats GBIF in both directions

`nativity_verdict` checks the review first. A reviewed introduction is refused
whatever its counts; a reviewed native survives GBIF's error **but still has
to clear the occurrence floor** — correcting the origin is not a free pass
through the rest of the gate, and a test pins that.

A species GBIF flags after this review was written is refused, and the reason
names the file to go and fix. Failing closed with a signpost, rather than
silently.

## Schema v68 — two photographs

`data/plant_photos.json` shipped as a literal `[]`. It now has two rows, and
one is a **habit shot of *Astragalus laxmannii*** — the backlog records that
zero species had one.

Without the bump both sit in the JSON and reach nobody: the
reseed-that-never-fired failure, which would be an odd one to hit on the file
whose entire purpose is being seen.

`add_photo` now collapses whitespace in the credit. The real import produced
`"(c) \nKENPEI, some rights reserved (CC BY)"`, and a credit is a single line
wherever it is shown — a stray newline renders as a broken caption under
somebody's photograph.

## Where the held edges stand

6,231 surviving edges, blocked on **1,194 animals** that still have no `fauna`
row. Applying writes nothing until those rows exist.

```
3723  no georeferenced records in AB or SK
2462  already seeded
 745  under 3 records in AB or SK — present, but too thin
 700  larval_host not from GloBI's explicit hostOf verb
 125  reviewed as introduced to Canada
  46  bird record the species' diet does not support
  43  bird reject by curation
  18  nectar claimed on a wind-pollinated plant
```

"Only flora and fauna native to AB and SK" costs **4,591 edges and 1,704
animals**, measured.

**The blocker is now naming, not nativity.** `fauna.common_name` is `NOT NULL`
and most of the 1,194 are solitary bees and micromoths with no common name in
any language. Three options — fall back to the scientific name, generate a
descriptive one, or admit only species that already have one — and it changes
what the app looks like, so it is the author's call. That is the next
increment and it is the last thing between here and 6,231 edges.

## What could not be verified here

- **That each of the 19 introductions is correct.** Unsourced calls from
  knowledge, like the 142 fauna rows already shipped, which is why the table
  is diffable with a reason per line.
- **That the 2,406 citations point at the right paper.** GloBI's
  study-to-record mapping is taken as given; only the shape is handled here.
- **The 46 edges still citing the aggregator.** Their pairs matched no
  observation record, and no fetch is going to change that.
