# V3.00 — One picker, one species page

*One item: **F192**, step 2 of the V2.98 review of perusing, selecting and placing
plants and communities: "One picker with one species page. Reuse the Directory's
widget-based page as the detail view inside the panel (or as a flyout), give it a
Place button, and delete the painted card. The Directory, the Browse tab and the
builder then share one list and one filter set. Clears 3, 5 and 7, and most of
17." The owner asked for the next step after V2.99: "Continue on the next step".*

**On the version number.** This is the first V3 release, on the owner's word ("You
should bump to V3.00"), given while the work was under way on a branch the convention
had named V2.100. The branch was renamed before anything was pushed. Two-digit minors
continue: `version_branch.next_version_branch` keeps the minor's zero-padding, so the
release after this one is V3.01, and everything that compares versions reads the two
numbers as integers, so the updater orders V3.00 above V2.99.

## What was wrong

Measured on V2.99 (256f75b), with the catalogue as shipped (424 species):

| Picker | Where | Filters | Detail view | Can place |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Browse** | Plants → Browse | 9 dropdowns + 4 toggles, 14 controls above the first plant | a card painted into the row (▶) | yes |
| **Plant Directory** | View → Plant Directory, and the start screen | 7 dropdowns + 13 toggles | a widget page beside the list | **no** |
| **Community builder** | Plant Communities → New / Edit | 1 checkbox + 4 single-choice dropdowns, its own code | none: rows read "Boreal Yarrow  (herb)" | n/a (it drops on a grid) |

A fourth, `AddMemberDialog` (a role dropdown over a 424-entry plant dropdown), has had
no caller since the builder replaced it; its note says it was "kept in case external
code or tests still import it". Nothing does.

The review's findings this step answers:

- **3. The card is painted.** `PlantRowDelegate` draws the detail block with
  QPainter into a row whose height is estimated in advance. Saskatoon Berry has 26
  animals recorded; the card lists 7, ends on "Andrena candida," and the next line
  draws over it. Nothing on it can be selected or copied, and the accessibility tree
  has nothing under the row.
- **5. The row's badges are never explained.** Hovering the dot, the zone chip, the
  "AB" chip or "[1×]" shows one tooltip, the plant's name. The type colours' only key
  is inside the Type dropdown.
- **7. Pond plants come first.** `search_plants` orders `plant_type, common_name`,
  and "aquatic" sorts first: Browse opens on Arum-leaved Arrowhead, Broad-leaved
  Arrowhead, Broad-leaved Water-plantain, Buckbean, Canada Waterweed, Cattail. The
  generator knows which plants are recorded near the pin (`src/site_fit.py`); no
  picker asks. The Directory has filters Browse lacks, and Browse has filters the
  Directory lacks (fruit month, ecoregion, perennial).
- **17 (most of it).** A plant row reads its common name and nothing else; the
  expanded card reads nothing; eight of nine filters have no name.

Two more from reading the code:

- **Five of the Directory's toggles are Browse's "Use" values under another name.**
  Keystone, Larval host, Bird food, Pollinator and Nitrogen fixer each run
  `_use_filter(<the same tag>)` in `search_plants`. Joining the vocabularies loses
  nothing; it removes five duplicates.
- **"Animals supported" order never worked.** `plant_directory.sort_species("wildlife")`
  sorts by `_wildlife_count`, a key nothing in the codebase sets, so it has always
  been A–Z. No control exposed it, which is why nobody saw.

## The change

### One species page, beside the list

`src/species_page.py` is the Directory's page, moved out of
`plant_directory_window.py` so that it can be shown in two places. The Directory
window shows it beside its list as before. The Browse tab shows it **beside the side
panel, over the right edge of the map** (`src/species_flyout.py`), rather than in
place of the list or squeezed under it:

- The list stays in view, so stepping through it with the arrow keys reads one page
  after another, which is the comparison the multi-expand card allowed.
- The map is not in use while you are reading, and the page closes the moment you
  place, so the yard is clear for the click.
- It is a sibling of the map in the map's column, the way the placement bar is, not
  a child of the web view: `QWebEngineView`'s accessible interface reports only the
  page (measured in V2.98), so a child would be invisible to a screen reader.

The page gains what the painted card had and it lacked: the plant's **roles** in
words (the tags the Role filter matches), how many are **in this design**, and the
months it **flowers and fruits** drawn as a twelve-month bar, whose accessible name
is the sentence (`phenology_bar.alt_text`). The card's planting-calendar strip is not
carried over as a strip: 98% of its 2,204 cells are "growing" or "harvest", which
restate the bloom and fruit windows; the months that say something else (pruning in
21 cells, sowing in 18) are a line of text. Wildlife lists every group's first eight
and a link to the rest ("and 18 more"), because Boreal Yarrow has 296 documented
animals.

### Selecting shows the page; placing closes it

V2.99 made selecting *look* rather than arm. This finishes the sentence the review
wrote for step 1, "Selecting shows details":

| Gesture in the plant list | Not placing | Placing |
|---|---|---|
| A finished click, an arrow key | names it on Place **and shows its page** | switches to it (unchanged); the page stays shut |
| → (Right arrow) | shows its page and moves focus into it | nothing |
| Right-click → About ‹plant› | the same | not offered |
| Enter, a double-click, Place, the page's Place | places it; the page closes | same |
| Esc in the page, or in the list while it is open | closes it; focus goes back to the list | stops placing (unchanged) |
| Tab focus arriving, a drag into the mix | nothing (unchanged) | nothing |

**No page opens while the map is placing.** The first version of this table let →
open one mid-placement, which would have covered the ground the next click needs and
the bar over it; placing owns the map until Done or Esc.

The ▶ chevron is gone with the card. Its 18 px went back to the plant's name.

### One list, one filter set

`src/plant_filters.py` holds the vocabulary once, Qt-free: **nine facets** (type,
sun, water, role, where to buy, restoring toward, blooms in, fruits in, flower
colour) and **nine on/off qualities** (native, perennial, edible, has a photo, feeds
a specialist, pet safe, child safe, well behaved, easy to find). It is the union of
the two pickers less the five duplicates. `src/plant_picker.py` is one widget, the
search box, the filters, the count and order, and the list, and all three pickers
are it:

- **Browse** (`plant_panel.py`) keeps its Place button, gestures, context menu and
  mix; the search and filter code leaves it.
- **The Directory** shows the filters open, and its page offers Place when a design
  is open, which hands the plant to the panel and brings the map forward.
- **The builder** gets the same list (with its natives-only default as the Native
  quality); its layer and function choices still lift matching plants to the top.

Eighteen controls do not fit above a list in a 437 px column, which already had 14
(finding 2). So the panel and the builder start with the filters **folded to one
line that says what is on**, "Filters · Restoring toward Aspen Parkland", with Clear
beside it; one click or Space opens them. That line also shows the restoring-toward
filter a dropped pin sets, which until now narrowed the list without saying so.
This is the seed of step 4, not step 4: labelled chips per filter, the combine logic
on screen and an empty state that names the blocking filter are still F194's.

### What was recorded near the site first

The order control offers **Recorded near this site** when there is a pin, and makes
it the default: species recorded near the pin first (`site_fit.locality`: here, then
near, then everything else level, as the generator ranks), then those hardy at the
site's zone, and plants that need standing water (`zoning.needs_standing_water`, 23
species) last. **Nothing is hidden** (P9): records follow collectors, and the order's
tooltip says so. Without a pin the default is Name. Type, Animals supported (now
counted) and Height are the others.

It was built as **Suits this site**, the review's words, and renamed after the live
check: at the example's Edmonton pin the list opened on Alkali Cord Grass, a plant of
saline flats recorded near Edmonton. Nothing here knows the yard's sun, soil or
moisture, only where a species has been recorded and the zone, so the name says that.

### What each part says about itself

Each badge gets its own tooltip (finding 5): the dot says the type, the zone chip
"Hardy in zones 3 to 9", the AB chip "Native to Alberta" or "Not native to
Alberta", the count "2 in this design". A row's accessible text carries the same
facts. Every filter has an accessible name and says its value.

### Native, as the flora records it

The Native quality reads `native_province = 'AB'`, the province list VASCAN supplies
(V2.80), not the seed's `native_to_alberta` flag. Five rows still carry the seed's
'1?' in the flag (False Box, Flat-topped White Aster, Round-leaved Alumroot, Stiff
Sunflower, Tall Anemone), VASCAN records all five in Alberta, and the list's AB badge
already called them native; the flag's filter dropped them from Browse and the
Directory, while the builder's own Python filter kept them. One filter set had to
pick one meaning, and the sourced one is the right one: 410 natives become 415. The
generator, its critic, the design goals and the API still filter on the flag, which
is a data fix (F199), not this step's.

## Found on the way

- **The Native filter dropped five species VASCAN records in Alberta.** Above; F199
  for the paths that still read the flag.
- **"Animals supported" never sorted anything.** `sort_species("wildlife")` read
  `_wildlife_count`, a key nothing set, so it was A–Z; no control offered it, so no one
  saw. It counts distinct animals now (`plant_filters.animals_per_plant`).
- **The page called relationships species.** "Feeds or shelters 334 documented
  species" on Boreal Yarrow, which has 334 relationships and 296 animals, because one
  bee can take nectar and pollen; 77 of the 289 species with animal records were
  over-counted. `species_entry` now carries `animals` and `specialist_animals` beside
  `total` and `specialists`, which stay relationships: the website calls them
  "documented animal relationships" and is right.
- **A fourth picker with no caller.** `AddMemberDialog`, a role dropdown over a
  424-entry plant dropdown, was "kept in case external code or tests still import
  it". Nothing did. Deleted with its `OffsetCanvas`, and the builder's `_truthy_int`
  and `condition_matches` import, which only its old filter used: 240 lines.
- **Tall photographs pushed the page below the fold.** A herbarium sheet scaled to
  the page's width stood 356 px tall; photos now fit inside 320 × 240 (live check).
- **The user guide's "Find a plant" had been wrong since V1.85**: it listed Medicinal,
  N-Fixer and Pollinator toggles that became Use values then, and said the region
  "persists across sessions", untrue since V1.87. Rewritten for this release.
- **`pyproject.toml` said version 1.41.** Nothing reads it (the app reads the branch
  through `version.txt`); it says 3.00 now.

## Left alone, on purpose

- **Communities.** The Plant Communities tab keeps its list and detail pane; step 6
  (F196) is about comparing them.
- **Filter readability beyond the folded line** (F194): chips, per-filter clear,
  combine logic, the empty state. And the site's **soil pH**, which `set_soil_ph`
  applies as a silent filter since V1.67; F194 is where a filter the user did not
  choose gets shown.
- **The rest of the accessibility baseline** (F195): focus styles, text size,
  contrast, target sizes, the single-letter shortcuts.
- **The mix extraction** that V2.99's note said this step needed first: the search
  and filter code leaving `plant_panel.py` freed more room than the mix would have
  (1599 → 1435 lines). The mix is still the next extraction.
- **Native follows Alberta wherever the pin is** (F200). The generator has followed
  the pin's province since V2.85; the pickers' Native and the AB badge do not yet.
- **The five '1?' flags themselves** (F199): a seed change with a schema bump, for the
  generator's sake, and out of a UI step's way.

## What could not be verified here

- **Screen readers, heard.** The names above are read from the accessibility tree,
  not listened to in Orca, NVDA or VoiceOver. Windows' UI Automation and macOS read
  the names Qt is given; that was not run.
- **Windows and macOS layout.** Arial-metric fonts here; a wider macOS font may wrap
  the nine quality chips onto a third row when the filters are unfolded.
- **Photographs over the network.** The container reaches no photo host, so the page
  showed photos already in the local cache and "Photograph not downloaded yet" for
  the rest; a download landing while a page is open redraws it (tested with a stub).
- **The '●' left after unticking** (V2.98 finding 6). A real-click untick left the box
  empty here; the sequence that leaves the mark is not known, and it is F194's.
