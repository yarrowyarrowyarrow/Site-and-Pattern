## What was built

- **`src/advanced_tools.py`** (Qt-free but for QSettings): `is_on`, `set_on` over
  `ui/advanced_tools`, and the registry: pages, menu actions (by object name),
  toolbar items, controls inside pages, and hooks (objects told the state through
  `set_advanced_tools`). Also `uses_advanced(project)` for MD-3.
- **`src/advanced_tools_flow.py`**: `apply(main)` walks the registry; `install` adds
  View → Advanced tools; `AdvancedBar` is MD-3's line, a sibling of the map in its
  column (the V2.98 rule), refreshed on open, undo, File › New and every switch.
- **Hidden in simple mode:** Site › Slope and Wind; Design › Habitat, Food, Over time
  and Water; Share › Present and the Pictures section of Export; the Learn tab; the
  View menu's windows, Scale Units and Map Settings; File › Import Yard Scan (deleted
  by RM-2 next increment); Measure and Note on the Draw row, and the View row's
  layer switches, Grid and Yard photo; Sun & Shade's arc group; Features beyond
  *Scan this area*; the plant mix, the community mix and the community builder's
  buttons; Row, Grid and Circle patterns; the plant page's Add to mix; the pattern
  card; Draw › Shape's lines and Custom; the right-click menus' mix entries; the
  letters M and N.
- **Measured** (`scripts/surface_inventory.py --mode`, the worked example, 1366 ×
  768): **75 controls on the pages in simple mode, 155 in advanced** (156 at V3.05).

## Decided, and why

- **The Report card stays in the simple app until the overview exists.** Part 02
  puts *Overview* there and the Report card behind the switch, but the overview is
  increment 10 (OV-1). Hidden now, simple mode would show only *Planted* for six
  increments. It moves behind the switch when OV-1 lands.
- **Pages, actions and controls, not just pages.** Several §5 surfaces are controls
  inside a page that stays (Sun & Shade's arc, Features' imports, the builders).
  Each is a container made for the purpose and registered, so nothing else that
  shows or hides the controls inside is overruled.
- **Features stays, with only *Scan this area*.** Part 02 moves the scan to Sun &
  Shade; moving widgets between pages is for the Site Info rewrite (WD-2), so the
  scan stays where it is and everything else on the page waits for the switch.
- **Draw › Shape keeps every area type.** Part 02 says "beds and paths" and MD-3
  counts other shapes as advanced, but V3.20's planting area is measured from lawn,
  patio and water shapes, which a homeowner needs to draw. Only the lines (hedge,
  fence, living fence, windbreak) and *Custom* wait for the switch, and the bar
  counts those.
- **A site photo is not advanced.** MD-3's list includes it, but it sits on Site ›
  Notes, which part 03 §6 keeps in the simple app, and 03 wins where they disagree.
  Wind overlays are not saved in a design, so the detector cannot count them.
- **The glossary test is a ratchet.** WD-1's own acceptance has it failing until
  WD-2 to WD-6 land. Shipping a red test would turn CI red for five increments, so
  it carries today's counts per module and word (34 entries, 72 strings), fails on
  any new use, and fails when a count drops until the list is lowered, as
  `data_quality.KNOWN_NOMENCLATURE` does. The command line, the MCP server, the API,
  the data gate, the AI prompts and the website are exempt, each with its reason.
- **Advanced-only modules in the pointer test.** The scan import dialog, the 3D
  window and the snow-catch flow are reached only through advanced tools; their
  pointers are read in advanced mode only.

## Found on the way

- The smoke suite walked hidden tabs; `_visual.every_tab` now skips them, so the
  simple run measures what simple mode shows and the advanced run (a subclass of the
  whole smoke class, with the switch read as on) measures everything.
- A tool the switch hides is not left armed: turning it off cancels Measure or a map
  note in progress, and a hidden pattern falls back to Single.
