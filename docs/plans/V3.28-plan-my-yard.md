## What was built

**One planting area** (PY-2). `yard_overview.BED_SHAPES` (Garden Bed, Mulch
Area, Lawn — to convert, the three Restoration stages) and `NOT_PLANTED` (Lawn
Area, Pathway, Patio / Deck, Water Feature, Existing remnant) are the one
vocabulary: `exclusion` reads its fill zones and avoided shapes from them, so
the plan plants exactly where the Overview measures. Plants are sized against
the plantable area (`_plantable_m2`), not the outline, which had put a 4.5 m Pin
Cherry in an 18 m² bed; the anchor grid gets finer when keep-out takes more
than a quarter of it, and a drift member is clipped to the beds.

**Fill to cover** (PY-4, `src/plan_cover.py`). After the main pass, the critic
and the top-ups, the plan adds its own low and middle plants (most numerous
first, then the pool's best-ranked; no trees, vines or water plants), each at
the open spot `OpenGround` finds, until the cover reaches the target: **70%**
for *Fill it now*, **40%** for *Over two or three summers*, 25% and 45% for the
advanced Sparse and Balanced. It stops at the target, at the plant ceiling or
when nothing finds open ground, and a plan that stops short says so in its
notes. Skipped when a budget is set, which trims instead. Each added plant's
page says "Added so the planting fills the ground you outlined".

**The dialog** (PY-1, PY-9). Named **Plan my yard** everywhere a person sees
it (File › Plan My Yard…, Ctrl+G; the strip's button; the status line; the
undo label). It opens whatever the state and says first what is missing:

| State | First line | Buttons |
|---|---|---|
| No pin | Find your yard first, so the plan can use your soil and sun. | Find my yard |
| A pin, nothing outlined | Outline where you want to plant: your whole yard, or just the beds. | Outline my yard · Draw a bed |
| Outside the provinces | the region's message (ED-8) | none |
| Ready | the questions | Plan my yard (Replace my plan / Add to my plan) |

The buttons run the Overview's own action targets (`yard_overview_flow`), so
*Draw a bed* opens Draw › Shape with Garden Bed chosen. Ready, it asks:
**Where** (the beds, with their count and area, only when beds exist; else
the yard outline, said, not asked), **How full** (each choice with its
estimated plants and cost, "about"), **Anything it must do?** (the five
must-haves word for word from §4.3, the first two ticked from the first-run
goals; the poison boxes' tooltips say most plants have no record and the
people box leaves out thorny plants too) and an optional **Budget**. Native
around Edmonton and food for pollinators are applied without a box (ED-10).
With the Advanced tools on it adds Sparse and Balanced as cover targets, *More
goals* (province-wide native, the local list, pollinators), the wildlife
picker and *Match to site conditions*. No AI anywhere.

**The estimate** (`plan_cover.estimate`). Plants overlap as they grow, so
cover rises as `1 − exp(−n·a/A)`: `n = −ln(1 − target)·A/a`, with a grown
spread `a` of 0.7 m² and $9–$19 a plant, both measured on the probe yard's
plans. It says about 260 and 110 where the plans placed 267 and 95; in one
18 m² bed about 30 and 13 where plans placed 20 to 74 and 11 to 34, depending
on the plants chosen. Rounded to 5 or 10, and the label says *about*.

**A bed is enough** (PY-10). With beds and no outline the beds' box is the
outline; a plan around the pin had placed 4 plants, it now places 20 at 70%
cover, all in the bed. The getting-started strip reads *Find your yard ·
Outline where to plant · Plan it, or pick plants yourself*, its button *Plan
my yard*, and a bed completes step 2.

## Deviations from part 07

- **Lawn — to convert is planted now.** V3.20 counted it with the lawn (not
  planted); a zone a person draws to convert is where they want plants, and
  §4.3 lists it with the beds. This reverses V3.20's reading.
- **The conversion-zone rule is cover, not plants a m².** §5 asked for as many
  plants a m² as the open yard, written when a zone collapsed a plan to six
  plants at 23%. Filling to cover, a tree and three shrubs cover more than
  thirty violets, so the probe asks for the zone's cover within 5 points of the
  open yard's, by at least six kinds.
- **Fill it now reaches 67%, not 70%.** The fill stops when four rounds of its
  plants find no spot that adds a tenth of their own spread; the rule (≥ 65%)
  holds.
- **Front or back yard is not asked** (PY-7, increment 12), nor *Try another*
  (PY-6) nor *About this plan* (PY-8). The Design notes box still opens after a
  plan, in both apps, until PY-8 replaces it.

## Could not be verified here

- The dialog on Windows or macOS: drawn offscreen in DejaVu Sans (CI's font),
  640 × 544, inside a 1366 × 768 screen; the strip needs 851 px of its 913.
- Whether 267 plants on 150 m² is a sensible "fill it now" to a nursery or a
  homeowner's wallet (about $2,300–$4,900 by the estimate). Part 06 §12 asked
  the same of the fill; the owner's call.
- A real plan with terrain and shade: the probe has none (no network), so
  every plant's reason is the no-site-data one.
