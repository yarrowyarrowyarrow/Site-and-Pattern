# V3.43 · The 3D preview, reviewed from real use, and the first of eight increments

*The owner, 11 November 2026: "I'd like a review of the 3D preview aspect of the app. I need you
to go in and actually test a design to see what the process and product is like. The user interface
and experience should also be assessed. Find out the greatest deficits to making it a useful,
realistic, and inspiring visual. I need a plan to address these deficits including quick and easy
wins but this needs to be thorough and create significant improvement. Then we can implement these
incrementally." And on the 12th: "ensure the work is saved and not half done", so friends can work
on it too.*

**This file is the handover.** It holds the review, the eight increments with what each is for,
the owner's decisions, what V3.43 built, and how to run the gate and the pictures. A person or a
session starting the next increment needs nothing that is not written here.

**It reverses a standing decision.** G4 (V3.17, `docs/homeowner/12-decisions.md`) froze the
preview: "no new 3D features; short of a crash or a wrong plant, won't fix". The owner's ask lifts
it for this programme.

## How it was tested

At V3.42, with nothing in the repo changed:

- Designs made the way the app makes them: the shipped example yard, and a 15.2 x 36.6 m lot in
  south Edmonton with a scanned house, garage, two neighbours and two existing trees, three beds,
  a path, a driveway, a patio and a lawn, planned twice by `generate_design_offline` with the
  first-run goals (front, then back): 365 plants of 16 kinds.
- The exact payloads `Scene3DWindow._push_scene` sends (`build_scene`, the animals, the cards)
  pushed into the real `html/scene3d.html` in headless Chromium: 50 pictures. Opening views, eye
  level, from above, three detail levels, years 1 to 25, six months, four hours, Walk, Fly,
  Identify, the cards, Flyover, the camera presets.
- The real app booted offscreen for the window's own controls
  (`scripts/surface_inventory.py --windows --mode advanced`).

**Not tested:** a real graphics card (software rendering changes light and edges, not shapes or
sizes), frame rate, Windows, QtWebEngine's own WebGL, the online path (terrain and wind could not
be fetched, so this is the offline user's flat yard).

**The pattern behind the findings.** Six audits (`docs/SPRITE_AUDIT.md`) rendered one plant at a
time, and the V3.37 use test could not open 3D at all. Nobody had rendered a whole yard from a
person's height. The plants got years of care; the yard around them (house, ground, street, light,
camera, timeline) got almost none, and that is where nearly all of the deficit is.

## What earlier increments learned, and every one of these must keep

- **Draw nothing invented.** V2.29's ground tufts were pulled because they "muddle the real plants
  from these fake ones". Ground is texture, never geometry; setting is pale and plainly not the
  design (P9).
- **Raise the whole scene together** (V2.34's uncanny valley): setting and light before more leaf
  detail.
- **Year 0 stays "fully grown" in the model.** Fix labels and consumers, not `effective_age`.
- **Decide once in Python, draw in the viewer** (V2.88 to V2.93): new facts ride in the scene as
  additive fields, no `SCENE_VERSION` bump, the docstring and `test_scene_contract` updated.
- **Anything lying on the ground asks `terrainHeightAt`** (V3.04, `test_slope_render`).
- **Chunks are at their ceilings:** `02-plants.js` 687/700, `04-quality.js` 896/900, `06-fly.js`
  941/950, `08-modes.js` 589/600 after this increment. New code goes in new chunks; anything the
  render loop calls from a later chunk needs a `typeof` guard; `test_js_names`,
  `test_bridge_contract`, and `test_leaf_light` after any change to light.
- **A probe that throws fails** (`probe_watch.js`, V3.37), and a new gate must be seen to fail on
  the fault it is for.
- **The simple app's rules hold in the 3D window:** the vocabulary ratchet, English animal names,
  12 px text, 24 px targets, named controls, both toolbars kept equal (`test_viewer_controls`). No
  plant-use text (P12).
- **No per-frame cost added blind:** the frame-rate readout below is run on the owner's laptop
  before an increment spends more.

**3D-2 · Getting there.** The door (M2, D1): "See it in 3D" on the plan strip (`plan_bar.py`) and
the Overview's street item (`yard_overview_items.py`, `yard_overview_flow.run_action`), with a step
in `test_homeowner_path`. A short toolbar by default through `scene3d_toolbar.py`'s `groups=`:
*When* (first summer to fully grown, named stops), *Season*, *Time of day*, *View* (From the
street · From the house · From above · Walk), Reset; the Advanced tools add the fine sliders,
Detail, Identify, the pollinator tools, Plant and Remove, the outputs, registered in
`advanced_tools.py`. Opens on the fifth summer, July, mid-afternoon, from the street (D5). Slider
pushes coalesced (one per 80 ms) and the succession result kept per design. A "from the house"
view beside `views.street` in `src/scene_views.py`. Card text to 12 px; the Creatures bar only
while animals are the subject; the default creature has an English name and plants in this design.

**3D-3 · Your yard.** Additive scene fields from a new Qt-free `src/scene_site.py`, one vocabulary
with `yard_overview.BED_SHAPES` / `NOT_PLANTED` and `shape_tool.AREA_PRESETS` (a test fails on a
shape type with no surface): `surfaces[]` (bed, mulch, lawn, lawn to convert, path, patio, water,
zones; a Custom shape keeps the person's colour), `lines[]` (hedge, fence, living fence,
windbreak), `street`, a roof hint. A new chunk draws surfaces as flat textured polygons draped on
the ground, fences as posts and boards, a pale sidewalk and road on the street side, houses as
plain massing (light walls, a darker roof with an overhang; a gable only on a clean four-corner
footprint). Where nothing is drawn (D3): lawn inside the outline, pale neutral ground outside it.
`test_slope_render` extended to surfaces; the yard probe checks a bed is not lawn-coloured.

**3D-4 · The timeline.** A season block decided once in Python (`src/scene_season.py`, from the
pack's frost dates in `region_climate`, labelled typical for the region): herbs emerge through
April and May, turn in October, stand as dry stems in winter (the app's own advice is to cut back
in spring); every deciduous woody plant drops its leaves alike; the October ground fixed.
`timeline_notes` in the scene ("46 of 56 plants in the bed by the spruce are shaded out by the
fifth summer") and a chip in the window that lists and rings them. V3.43's HUD already counts
them; this says where and which.

**3D-5 · The planner and shade (D4).** `_zone_context` builds its shade grid on a flat 1 to 2 m
grid over the outline when there is no terrain (`shade_grid_for_design(..., terrain=False)`), and
uses that finer grid for shade when there is. Held by `probe_plan_my_yard.py --assert`: *a plan
survives its own preview*, under 10% of a fresh plan shaded out by year 5 on the review lot. It
changes what the planner picks, so it is measured and shown first, as V3.25 did for the score.

**3D-6 · Light (D6).** Sky and ground light that follow the sun's height, the ground bounce
(F187), exposure that lifts at low sun and at night, the shadow map fitted to the yard plus its
casters' reach, soft contact shade under beds. Measured with V2.94's near-black share over fixed
views and hours in the yard probe; the owner approves from a before/after sheet.

**3D-7 · Trees and beds.** Triangle budget by crown size so a 14 m tree's leaf cards stay near
life size, round trunks, the `def_*` models redone; herb models with basal leaf mass (F180's "bare
stems"); far herbs on the cheaper unit; Balanced and Lifelike made to differ on purpose (D7).

**3D-8 · The laptop.** F211's software path with a message that says it is the slow one; detail
chosen from the measured frame rate; 60 frames only while an animal is in view.

**Not in this programme** (data or art first): insect bodies F173, creature looks F114/F118,
per-species tuning F180, animal photos F257, terrain past the boundary F204, photo-textured
foliage (D2).

## What V3.43 built (3D-1)

**A gate that sees a whole yard.** `tests/test_yard_render.py` builds a yard through the real
`build_scene` (a scanned house, a drawn tree crown, a boulevard tree, plants the timeline shades
out and lives out), pushes it into the real viewer in headless Chromium and reads the whole picture
back through `html/yard_probe.html`: 18 checks in about five seconds. **Against V3.42's viewer 12
of them fail** (`YARD_VIEWER_ROOT=<a V3.42 checkout>/html`), which is the proof it sees the
faults. `scripts/render_yard.py` draws the contact sheets.

**Buildings are buildings.** The scene sends a footprint as a canopy only when it says it is a
tree's crown (`caster_kind`, as `shade.py` reads it; a shape drawn as "Tree canopy" before the
marker existed counts too). A test holds the scene and the shade engine to one answer.

**The camera frames the yard.** `src/scene_views.py` decides two things once, for the orbit, the
stills and the walker alike. `focus` is the design's own extent (outline, placed plants,
structures, padded 2 m, at least 8 m, no ±25 m floor); the orbit, Reset view, the Flyover and
leaving Walk or Fly frame it (`frameBox()`), and a scene without one frames the stage as before.
`views.street` is where a neighbour stands: 4 m outside the edge facing the street
(`src/frontage.py`: the person's answer, else measured from the roads, else south), looking at the
planting on that side, stepped sideways, nearer or further until no trunk stands between the
sidewalk and the yard and the eye is in no building. The sidewalk camera, the in-yard camera and
the walker's start use it. **It is the same spot in every year**, worked out from the design as
planted (a plant shade has taken still counts, a shrub by its grown height, a self-seeded recruit
not at all): the before/after page renders it at three years and its panels only compare from one
place. The sprite gallery keeps its own stage.

**The walker gets past the house.** A building was a circle to him, 90% of the way to its
farthest corner: on the review lot 7.3 m of radius on a lot 7.6 m to each side, so both side yards
were closed. It stops him at its walls now (`ringNearest`); he walks under a drawn canopy.

**What stands.** `presence_opacity` is one number with two meanings, and the viewer drew both by
blending the plant toward the sky. In 3D a late-successional plant "starting faint" is drawn whole
(a young oak is all there, only small), and a short-lived kind "fading to a remnant" is **fewer
plants**: a fixed number per plant against the falling share, so a plant gone one year is gone
every year after. `gone` says why a placed plant is not drawn (`shade`, `short_lived`);
`scene_contract.standing_plants()` is the one read side. **Found on the way:** the animals and the
cards read every plant in the scene, so a bee was sent to a plant the same scene had marked dead,
and "recorded as food for N kinds" counted plants no longer there. They, the spotlight and split
view read `standing_plants()` now; in the page `standing()` keeps a gone plant from being drawn,
clicked or walked into. A declining plant fades toward straw.

**Year 0 said truthfully.** The line at the top reads "Fully grown · June · 365 plants · 2 trees
already here", and in a later year "Year 5 · July · 219 plants · 2 trees already here · 136 lost
to shade · 10 short-lived, gone". The slider's label says *fully grown*. Without a photograph the
before/after page is three summers (first, one between, last), never year 0, and says "over 5
summers". The Flyover starts in the first summer and shows no year 0: ending on it would stand
every lost plant back up.

**Fruit.** A size per fruit form at no more than three times life (3 cm for a berry, 16 cm for a
hanging bunch; they were 17 to 34 cm), more of the small ones per plant. Estimates by form (D8).

**How fast it draws.** Ctrl+Shift+F in the 3D window shows frames a second, the worst frame,
triangles, draw calls, plants, the detail level and the picture's size (`26-perf.js`;
`?perf=1` for a probe). It wraps the render call only while showing.

**A test that failed by the calendar.** The baseline run on untouched V3.42 failed one test:
`test_sun_scrub…asks_for_a_redraw` picks Winter Solstice, which is already the panel's choice from
6 November to 3 February, so nothing redrew. It failed 90 days a year, for everyone. Fixed.

### Corrections made while building

- *No ghosts* was planned as a straw tint. That would have drawn a newly planted oak 80%
  dead-coloured, so 3D-4's "fewer, not paler" was pulled forward.
- The fruit needed no per-plant cap once the sizes were near life.
- The readout needed a key: an address flag cannot be typed in the app.
- The street view first moved by 10 to 30 cm between years as plants died; found by printing it for
  years 1, 5 and 25, and held by a test.

### Left alone

- The opening state is still fully grown, June, 1 pm (D5 is 3D-2's).
- Buildings are solid boxes. Roofs and walls are 3D-3's.
- `Growth Snapshots` still says "Mature" for year 0, and the wild-landscape walk "now": different
  windows, and for the walk "now" is right.
- The legacy `permaSetPlants` hook still receives raw `presence_opacity`; nothing in the app sends it.

### Not verified

- A real graphics card, Windows, and frame rate: the readout exists so the owner can read it off.
- The walker's facing when the street is not to the south: his position and heading are tested,
  the turn of his body was only seen from the south.

## Running it

```bash
QTWEBENGINE_CHROMIUM_FLAGS=--no-sandbox QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen \
  python scripts/run_tests.py --exclude tests.test_undo_redo --max-skips 25
```

The whole suite. Read its `RESULT:` line; the baseline on V3.42 was 5,569 tests, 14 skipped, and
the one calendar failure above.

```bash
python -m unittest tests.test_yard_render tests.test_scene_views tests.test_scene_contract
```

The gate and the scene's rules, about ten seconds. A skip saying "no WebGL" means the browser could
not draw; a failure naming the viewer means it threw.

```bash
python scripts/render_yard.py --out /tmp/yard
```

Plans the review lot and writes `yard.jpg` and `time.jpg` (a few minutes, drawn in software).
`--design my.perma.geojson` draws a saved design instead; `--viewer-root <other checkout>/html`
draws the same scenes with an older viewer.
