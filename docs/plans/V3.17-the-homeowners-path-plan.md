# V3.17 — A review, then a plan for the homeowner's path (docs only)

*The owner, first: "I need a full thorough user experience directed review to
analyze what remains to be done to make this polished to a point I could
literally sell it… do not do any coding til the review is complete." Then, on
reading it: "lets do a second pass… make the homeowner's path the strongest
aspect of the app… how to make it simpler… what it would mean for this app to
be local to just Edmonton… I will launch into the actual work following this
second pass… with a friend so the plan needs to be fully accessible from the
repository." No code changed in either pass.*

This file records how the two documents were made and what was decided while
making them. The documents themselves are:

- **The first pass:** [`../SALE_READINESS_REVIEW.md`](../SALE_READINESS_REVIEW.md),
  findings R1–R40.
- **The second pass, the plan:** [`../homeowner/README.md`](../homeowner/README.md)
  and its twelve parts.

Both rode the V3.17 branch after the icon (F232) had shipped and been released.
Every commit was docs-only and carried `[skip ci]`, so no release workflow
re-ran against `release-V3.17` (the V2.81/V2.82 lesson in CLAUDE.md).

## How the first pass was done

- **The real window, driven.** `scripts/surface_inventory.py` under a virtual
  display (1366 × 768, Liberation Sans) clicked every enabled button and opened
  every window and dialog; a scripted first hour (pin, outline, plants,
  generate, export) and an edge-case script (offline, a Toronto pin, a
  self-crossing outline, a damaged file, generating twice) ran against a
  throwaway database.
- **Two environment notes, for whoever repeats it:** the xcb platform plugin
  needs `libxcb-cursor0` and friends installed, and the map is black in screen
  grabs unless `QTWEBENGINE_CHROMIUM_FLAGS` includes `--disable-gpu`.
- **The suite, the CI way:** 4,754 tests OK, 12 skipped, with
  `tests.test_undo_redo` excluded as CLAUDE.md describes.

## How the second pass was done

Nine investigations. Four (the start screen, the plant ranking, the overview,
the Edmonton edition) ran as parallel agents with written briefs; their drafts
were read, checked against the code, edited and adopted as parts 04, 05, 06 and
08. The other five (what goes, Plan My Yard, personas, words, finishing) were
done directly in the session, each committed as soon as it was written so a
usage limit could not cost written work. Then the work plan, the decisions and
the start-here page.

**Measured, not assumed,** where it mattered:

- The generator's handling of drawn shapes, by a probe now committed as
  `docs/homeowner/prototypes/plan_my_yard_probe.py` (07 §2): a Garden Bed is
  ignored, a Lawn Area is planted, and a path or patio is kept out as one
  circle around its centre.
- The undefined-name guard, run against the tree: one failure, the Lessons
  door's `_sandbox_plants` (04 §7).
- Feature sizes and coupling for 03, by counting lines, importers and test
  modules, then reading each deletion's touch points by hand.
- Jargon in prose strings for 09, roughly; the proposed test does it properly.
- The repository's licence (PolyForm Noncommercial) against PyQt6's (GPL v3),
  the outside services each `src/` URL points at, and the release history
  (five full releases on 8 October 2026).

## Decided while writing

- **Decision letters were renamed to avoid collisions.** Part 05's decisions
  were P1–P10, which collided with the design principles P1–P13 cited in the
  same file: now L1–L10. The Edmonton draft's work items W1–W16 and defects
  N1–N7 became ED-1 to ED-16 and EDF-1 to EDF-7, leaving W for the words.
- **PR-5 (score tips by ranking) is superseded by OV-11**, which retires the
  tips; it stays in part 05 for the record and is not scheduled.
- **RM-3 (the AI leaves the app) does not wait for the Plan My Yard dialog**;
  it removes the AI from today's dialog, and PY-1 rebuilds it later.
- **Feature IDs F233–F249 are reserved** in `ROADMAP_NEXT.md` and `BACKLOG.md`
  (group S) in the same commit as this file, one per feature or data job rather
  than one per work item; next free is F250.
- **The work is cut at "must" and "should"** so the owner can stop early and
  still stop somewhere coherent (11 §3).

## Corrected on the way

- "Dry summers" for Edmonton, in an early draft: wrong. June and July are the
  wettest months in the shipped normal (87.1 and 91.7 mm).
- A first reading that the macOS release notes wrongly mentioned SmartScreen:
  the two workflows deliberately share one body. Withdrawn.
- Part 03 first said the start screen had "five near-identical rows" and Share
  "eight buttons"; the code says up to four rows and six buttons in two groups.
  Corrected before it was relied on.

## Left alone, on purpose

- **No code.** The owner asked for none until the plan was done.
- **V2.79's *Handing this over* and the V3.12 Edmonton rules** are not reopened.
- **P12.** Nothing is encoded; the one place the plan touches it (the desktop
  plant page's free-text notes, decision L8) recommends showing less.
- **The owner's V3.05 choices** (every *Keep* in the surface audit) are kept;
  pages move behind the Advanced switch rather than going. Where a
  recommendation reverses an earlier choice, 12 §4 lists it.

  public pages as summarised by search.
