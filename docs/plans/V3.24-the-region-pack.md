# V3.24 — The region pack (the homeowner plan's increment 7)

*The owner: "I believe garden centre plants are a misnomer as I recently
learned not many native plants at all are available at garden centres. There
are really only a few native plant retailers in Edmonton/Alberta. As for the
one liner I want it to list common natives, for example sneeze weed is not one
I am familiar with. Go ahead with the next increment." Increment 7 of
[`homeowner/11-work-plan.md`](../homeowner/11-work-plan.md): Edmonton as a
region pack ([part 08](../homeowner/08-edmonton-edition.md) §4), ED-1 to ED-5
and ED-11 (ED-13's installer half shipped in V3.22). Feature F237, which stays
open for increment 8. Decisions E1 (the 50 km circle), E4 (one installer, the
pin choosing) and E9 (rename communities by habitat, not yet done) took the
recommendation.*

## The owner's two corrections

- **No tier names a shop.** The availability tiers were called *Big-box store*
  and *Garden centre*, and the plant page said "usually stocked by garden
  centres". In the owner's experience few natives are sold there at all. The
  tiers now say how widely a plant is sold (*Widely sold*, *Often sold*,
  *Native-plant nurseries*, *Seed / plug only*, *Rare / hard to find*) in the
  filter, on the plant page, in the order file and in the website's sentence.
  The data under them (`availability_class`, `scripts/rank_availability.py`)
  is unchanged: it ranks how easy a plant is to get, which is still what the
  generator wants. Whether the tiers themselves should change is the owner's
  call, asked below.
- **The flowering line names familiar plants.** It now ranks by how often
  people within the region's circle have recorded a plant (its observations in
  the reviewed list: Fireweed 588, Red Osier Dogwood 964, Sneezeweed 1), and
  names at most two, counting the rest. October reads "Smooth Aster, Willow
  Aster and 1 more".

## What was built

- **`src/region.py`**: the packs, the default (`data/edition.json`), `at`,
  `coverage`, `for_site`, `message` (§4.5's wording), `place_line`, and the
  paths to a pack's list and rulings. Validation refuses unknown fields, so
  nothing about plant uses or cultural knowledge can be added in passing (P12).
- **`data/regions/edmonton/`**: `region.json` (§4.2, without the rules E6
  dropped), and the local flora list and rulings, moved.
- **ED-3**: the goal is `local_native`, built per pack by
  `design_goals.local_goal` and resolved against the pin, so the generator,
  the dialog and the critic all read the pin's pack. The picker's quality,
  the communities switch and the community page's line are named by the
  region.
- **ED-4**: `nurseries.region_nurseries` and `in_region`; the order file
  filters each channel's suppliers the same way.
- **The worked example** is read from the pack's manifest.

## Decided, and why

- **The list keeps its file format.** One file per pack, holding the same
  `places` document, so the move changed one line of 442 and the derivation,
  the review tool and the data gate needed only their paths.
- **The PDF's place line keeps the pin's address** (V3.18 already fixed
  EDF-2) and falls back to the region's name, then the coordinates.
- **The guard is a ratchet.** Part 08 counted 188 lines in 26 files; earlier
  increments and this one brought it to 15 uses in 5 files, all of them
  climate, address search or the Water page, which are increment 8's (ED-6,
  ED-7). The glossary and the website's facet modules are allowed with their
  reasons.
- **The two Calgary-area suppliers stay on Edmonton's list** (ALCLA Native
  Plants, Wild About Flowers), as part 08 has it: they ship, and say so
  ("ships to you").

## Left alone

- ED-6 to ED-10 (climate, address search, coverage messages in the UI,
  nativity by province, the region's defaults on) are increment 8.
- E9: the seven "Edmonton …" community names are seeded data; renaming them
  needs a schema bump and the owner's eye on the new names.
- The website reads the pack only through `native_here`; its own Edmonton
  pages are G5's (no website work for now).
