## What was done, measured and decided

- **OV-3.** `src/yard_overview.py` is new, holding only part 06 §5.1's planting area
  for now (OV-1 grows it): the union of bed-like shapes, else the boundary less lawn,
  path, patio, water and buildings. The five copies of "sum every drawn shape" (two
  in `app.py`, three in `pdf_export.py`) call `bed_area_m2`. Mulch is costed at the
  site-prep sheet's 5–8 cm, read from `site_prep.MULCH_DEPTH_CM`, where the cost had
  used 7.5 cm. **Decided:** *Lawn — to convert* counts as lawn too (it is lawn until
  it is converted). Without shapely the same rule runs on the shapes' stored areas,
  overlaps counted twice; shapely is in `requirements.txt`, but this container did not
  have it, which is how the fallback was found wanting (it had ignored the holes).
- **OV-4.** `habitat_score.score_for_project` (with `site_pin` and `ecoregion_at`)
  scores against the pin's ecoregion, as Habitat did; the Report card, the PDF, the
  design critic and the API all call it. The worked example moves **63 → 64** (Aspen
  Parkland's own layers). The critic now sees the rebased layer component, which can
  change which repairs a generated design gets; the full suite is the check for any
  generator test that moved, and none was regenerated.
- **OV-5.** `what_it_feeds.food_counts` and `food_words`: food edges only, each animal
  once. The Report card ("recorded as food for N kinds of wildlife"), Habitat
  ("Recorded as food for N kinds", by group), Food's headline and Planted's line
  ("food for 137 kinds: …") all use it. `HabitatScore.fauna_by_taxon` stays for the API
  and the critic, its comment now saying it counts any record. The example: **559**
  food against **558** by any record (the plan's 589 predates V3.18's plant swaps).
- **OV-6.** Over time › Effort renders `build_maintenance_calendar`, the PDF's own
  function: Year 1 by type (`maintenance_calendar.year1_by_type`, the band's
  arithmetic per type), then the four bands as ranges, a year and a week from May to
  September (÷ 22, `GROWING_WEEKS`). The example's Year 1 reads **36–76 h** on both.
  "Year 3+" and its multipliers are gone. The two tables were merged into one after a
  render showed their columns did not line up.
- **OV-7.** `src/water_budget.py` holds the constants and arithmetic the page had.
  Every type present has a row, in the plant key's order; rainfall is the site's
  fetched normals when present, labelled with their source, else "Edmonton normal";
  the surplus and deficit callouts are replaced by the weekly rule. The example:
  **2,937 L** in the first summer, **about 130 L a week**, both as part 06 measured.
  The planning panel is told the site config on open, undo and every site-data fetch.
- **OV-8.** *This month* and the status bar say harvest only of a plant with edible
  parts, and the care calendar's first-year line for the month ("If you planted this
  year:"). **Decided differently from the work item's wording:** the item says drop
  pruning; measured, the catalogue's pruning rows are 17 in March and April (shrubs,
  agreeing with the care calendar's "cut back in spring") and 4 in September and
  October. Only autumn pruning is dropped (`maintenance_calendar.says_prune`, months
  1–5), and the plant page follows the same rule, so it no longer says "Prune in
  October" either. The accept line holds; the owner may prefer dropping it entirely.
- **OV-11.** `reference_fidelity._tokens` keeps a common name whole and adds the
  genus only for a scientific name: the example's matches are six real species
  (Blanketflower, Chokecherry, Red Osier Dogwood, Saskatoon Berry, Stiff Goldenrod,
  Wild Bergamot), none of them "Prairie" or "Wild". The Tallamy line says
  caterpillars, with the citation. Tips and the light/shade mix are gone (with
  `set_shade_breakdown` and its two callers); "vs. lawn" reads "Against the lawn it
  replaces". Part 06 §7 also retires the Report card's "What your spend creates",
  done here. `analysis_panel.py` went from 2,078 to 1,810 lines and its ceiling in
  the architecture guard was lowered to 1,900.

## The full run

5,030 tests, the CI way: four failed. Three pinned the old numbers this increment
changed on purpose (mulch at 7.5 cm, twice; the Effort page importing the hours
constants rather than rendering the calendar) and were updated. The fourth,
`test_edit_menu`'s Ctrl+A test, **was V3.19's, not this increment's**: it fails the
same way on V3.19's last commit with the 54 modules that run before it, and passes
alone. Offscreen, the window shown last is the active one, and earlier tests leave
top-level labels that get shown while the test waits for the map; the main window
then holds neither the focus nor its shortcuts. The test now takes the window back
before pressing keys. The leaked labels were not traced to their test: a trace of every
label Python creates and shows caught none of them, so Qt made them (one held a
plant page's "Flower detail" line). A test-hygiene question for later; nothing
points at the app.
