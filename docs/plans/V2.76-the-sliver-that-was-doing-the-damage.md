# V2.76 — the sliver that was doing the damage

Carry the corrected occurrence data into the app, and say plainly on the
website what was wrong with the old numbers.

---

## Context

V2.75 fixed a derivation and could not fix the data. `ranges_for_species` had
inherited `ecoregion._NEAR_BOUNDARY_M` — a 5 km proximity buffer written in
V2.67 for *which ecoregion is this yard in*, where a nearby second answer is
genuinely useful — and had been crediting every occurrence record to every
region within five kilometres of it. The code was corrected; the shipped counts
could only be corrected by re-fetching ~400,000 records from GBIF, which needs
egress this container does not have. `/method/` disclosed the gap rather than
hiding it.

The author ran the fetch on 2026-08-21. This increment is what came back, and
one thing in it was not what anyone predicted.

## The error was concentrated, not scattered

V2.75 measured the *geometry*: over 4,000 random points inside the layer, 16.4%
fall within 5 km of a second region. That number is true and it turned out to
be the wrong thing to lead with.

`scripts/plot_occurrences.py --buffer-artefacts` measured the *outcome* on real
records: **493 of 4,218 region claims (11.7%)** had fewer than the floor of
records actually inside the region. And almost all of them were **one region**.

`western_continental_ranges` is the British Columbia interior ranges. Only a
hairline of it crosses into Alberta — **0.0345 deg², 0.02% of the mapped area,
36× smaller than Northern Continental Divide** and 815× smaller than Mid-Boreal
Uplands. A sliver that thin has a buffer-to-area ratio in the dozens, so its
5 km apron was sweeping in nearly every montane record near the provincial
border.

It was claimed by **135 species. It is now claimed by 15.**

This was checked rather than assumed: the region's own computed interior point
returns `['western_continental_ranges']` under containment, so the polygon is
matchable and the collapse is real rather than a geometry bug in the fix.

## What the re-derivation moved

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| species with at least one region | 422 | **420** |
| region rows | 4,215 | **3,643** |
| attributed records | 489,546 | **361,447** |
| `western_continental_ranges` | 135 species | **15** |
| `fescue_grassland` | 317 | 264 |
| `cypress_upland` (interior) | 231 | **228** |
| `selwyn_lake_upland` (interior) | 10 | **8** |

**The interior regions barely moving is the confirmation.** A boundary fix
should bite at edges and leave the middle alone, and that is exactly the shape
of the diff. Cypress Upland is an isolated plateau; it lost three rows.

## Two species lost every region, and that is the honest answer

*Panicum virgatum* (Switchgrass, 3 records) and *Symphyotrichum campestre*
(Western Meadow Aster, 6) were sitting at the floor on borrowed records. Both
now band **`unknown` — "Not enough to say"** rather than `unlikely`, because
`src/confidence.py` refuses a middle rung without evidence: under-collected and
absent are indistinguishable below the floor, and printing the second would
steer a real planting away from a species that may well belong.

Neither disappears from the site — both keep their heuristic ecozone tag, so
they still appear under Prairies. And the V2.75 gate noticed unprompted: its
nativity-evidence warning went **9 rows → 11**.

## What shipped

**`_SCHEMA_VERSION` 76 → 77.** No DDL; a reseed, and the first time this
catalogue has *corrected* a number rather than added one. Without the bump the
corrected ranges reach nobody who already has the app.

**`/method/` says what happened.** The "known limits" bullet claiming the counts
still carry the buffer became false and was replaced with the correction and its
size, plus a second bullet naming the sliver in plain language — *"If you
compared this site against an earlier copy of it, that is the difference you
would see."* A page about honesty that quietly restated its numbers would have
been the exact fault the V2.75 review was about.

`DATA_SOURCES.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and the backlog row were carrying the same
now-false sentence and were corrected with it.

## Checked and deliberately not changed

- **The legend does not link to pages that do not exist.** `interlake_plain` is
  the one region with zero species (it is a Manitoba ecoregion clipped to a
  fragment). `static_site_regions.link()` already returns `""` for any region
  with no hub page, so it renders as unlinked text — a place that exists with
  nothing recorded in it, which is true. `test_every_internal_link_resolves`
  covers ~9,600 links and would have caught the alternative.
- **The point cache is committed** (`data/fetched/plant_occurrences.json`,
  555,477 records, 17.9 MB). It is a dev artefact the app never reads. It is
  also why the *next* question about this derivation costs no network — the
  absence of it is why V2.75 could diagnose this bug and not fix it.

## Known, not fixed here

The **North America inset** and the **phenology bar** that regional floras draw
beside a dot map are both now within reach and neither is in this increment.
The inset needs a continental fetch (feasible by faceting on state/province
rather than harvesting points); the bar needs `bloom_period`, which is already
parsed for 428 species.

**Publishing the dots** is still blocked on licence and on rare-taxa location
obscuring — but a route opened: the cache carries `basisOfRecord`, and the
printed floras plot *herbarium specimen records*, which do not carry the
obscuring problem. "Publish `PRESERVED_SPECIMEN` only, and say so" is what the
reference works themselves do.
