# V3.11 — Measurements in the design, a switch for the corner handles, and a legend of what is drawn (F215–F217)

*The owner, 6 October 2026: "I noticed undo does not work for measurements, they
also do not get selected when I go to select an area. Also the dots on the corner
points of a boundary to move it around should be able to toggle on and off just
like the area and side length label. The legend needs work … I'd like to be able
to have degrees to the legend … I'd like to be able to type in 'city park land'
and 'private lot' and distinguish them via the legend. It would also be great if
the plants on the legend had the option to be simple (ie. tree, shrub, etc.) or
go down to the species level. This would have to be a bit more automatic than the
boundaries that I want more control over. I also liked the idea of hiding anything
from the legend that does not appear on the map as those are superfluous and
distracting."*

The owner remembered the legend's contents being discussed in the app
simplification review. Neither the audit (`docs/SURFACE_AUDIT.md`) nor the
decisions recorded on its page mention the legend; the only written rule is
V3.03's, in `docs/UI_PRINCIPLES.md`: "the legend names only what is drawn",
which then meant what the map *can* draw. This release reads it as what the map
*is* drawing, which is what the owner asked for.

## What was wrong (read in the code, then pressed in Chromium)

**A measurement lived only in the page.** `handleMeasureClick` (`04-tools.js`)
drew the line and told Python nothing: no bridge call, no feature. Undo is
Python's (a snapshot of `project["features"]`, V1.81), so it could not see a
measurement, and worse, Ctrl+Z straight after measuring undid whatever had been
done *before* it. Nothing removed them either: `clearAll()` leaves them and
`clear_measure()` had no caller in Python, so a new design, an opened design and
every undo's redraw kept the last design's measurements on the map. They were
never saved.

**The selection box had no case for them.** `_marqueeHitTest`,
`_sameSelectable`, `_refreshSelectionVisuals` and `deleteSelected` (`01-core.js`)
know plants, boundaries, structures, shapes and the sun path. And deleting a
mixed selection sent one bridge call per kind, each its own undo step: five
plants and two boundaries took three presses of Ctrl+Z to bring back.

**The corner handles came up on every press.** Since F213 (V3.11) a press
anywhere inside a boundary opens its handles (a blue dot per corner, an orange
one per corner of its box). That is what the owner asked for then; with plants in
the yard, it also means most clicks on open ground bring up nine dots. The
right-click menu could switch the edge and area labels per boundary, and nothing
could switch the handles.

**The legend was a fixed list.** Every entry showed whether or not anything of
its kind was on the map. The plant section listed all eleven types (V3.03). And
the Structures section was not true: it claimed category colours, but each
structure is drawn in its own colour (`src/db/structures.py`): its yellow
"Animal" matches no structure at all (there is no Animal category; the nearest
is Habitat), and its "Growing" brown `#8d6e63` is also the fill of the Native Bee
Habitat Log, a Habitat structure. Existing trees and buildings had no entry, and
every kind of shape shared one grey "Custom shape" line.

## What was decided

### F215 — a measurement is part of the design

A measurement becomes a feature like a note: `element_type: "measurement"`, a
two-point `LineString`, a `measurement_id`. The page tells Python when one is
made (`onMeasurementAdded`) or removed (`onMeasurementsRemoved`, one call for any
number); the handlers are `@undoable`, so undo, redo, save and reopen come from
the machinery every other feature uses, and the redraw after an undo clears and
reloads them (`render_project_to_map`). The design file's format goes 1.9 → 1.10,
additive (older readers skip unknown element types, as V1.48 and V2.05 relied
on). The View bar's Measurement switch stays a view setting, not an edit.

- **Saved with the design** is the behaviour change here, and the reason: a
  measurement drawn on a plan is a dimension line, and a dimension line that
  vanishes on reopening, or survives into the next design, is the bug. The
  owner did not ask for it; it falls out of making undo honest, and the reply
  says so.
- **Selectable**: the box catches a measurement when an end or its middle (where
  its label is) is inside; Shift-click toggles it; selected, it is drawn solid
  and thicker, since the selection's yellow is already the measurement's colour.
- **One undo step for one delete**: `deleteSelected` now sends everything it
  removed in one call (`onSelectionDeleted`), and Python runs the existing
  per-kind handlers inside one checkpoint. The handlers are unchanged, so what
  each removal does is unchanged; only the count of undo steps is.
- **Found on the way**: a first click in measure mode left its yellow dot on the
  map after Esc, and the next measurement started from it. Any mode change now
  drops a half-made measurement. And a new design kept the last design's map
  notes on the map (`clearAll` leaves them, as the skill says it does); New now
  clears them, as it now clears measurements.

### F216 — the corner handles switch per boundary

The boundary's right-click menu gains **Corner Handles**, beside Edge Labels and
Area Label, saved on the boundary (`show_handles`, default on) as they are. On:
a press shows the handles, as F213 made it. Off: a press shows nothing and the
boundary cannot be dragged out of shape by accident; the right-click menu, a
Shift-click selection and the box still reach it. Switching it on from the menu
shows the handles straight away, the way switching a label on shows the label.

**Not chosen:** handles that stay up until switched off. That reading of "just
like the labels" would either leave every boundary in edit mode or make F213's
press do nothing by default, three days after the owner asked for the press.

### F217 — the legend names what is on the map, at the detail asked for

The legend is built from what is drawn, every time the map changes (Leaflet's
own `layeradd`/`layerremove` on the map, debounced; only while the legend is
open). A kind of thing appears only when something of it is on the map and
shown: hide the boundaries with the View bar and "Boundary" leaves the legend.
With nothing drawn it says so. It lives in its own script, `12-legend.js`, as a
pure model (`legendModel`, from a plain snapshot of the map), a renderer and the
small part that reads the map, so node can test the first two.

- **Boundaries: Simple or Named**, a switch on the section. Simple: one line,
  "Boundary", with a swatch per colour in use. Named: one line per colour and
  name, unnamed ones reading "Boundary"; a click on a line asks for its name
  (Python's dialog, the way a map note is asked for, so it has a proper title and
  no web-page prompt), and names every boundary on that line, so naming the blue
  ones "City park land" is one step and one undo. The boundary's menu has
  **Name…** for one boundary. Names are saved on the boundary (`name`).
- **Plants: Type or Species**, a switch on the section. Type: the types on the
  map, in the Type filter's order, and a plant you gave its own colour listed by
  name, since no type explains its colour. Species: every species on the map,
  numbered, with its colour and how many are placed, **and the number drawn on
  each of its plants** while the legend is open, where a plant is big enough on
  screen to hold it.
- **Why numbers and not a colour per species.** V3.03 settled on one colour
  scheme for plants, by type, and V2.69 measured why identity below a parent
  should be a number: lightness steps collapse at about ten siblings (ΔE 0.3).
  The worked example alone has fourteen species. The numbers are the printed
  planting plan's (`planting_map._numbering`: by common name, then id), which
  keys them to the buy list, so a 7 on screen, on the planting map and on the
  buy list is the same species; a test runs the page's numbering against
  Python's.
- **Structures** are listed as they are drawn: each kind by its name and icon in
  its own colours, existing trees by conifer or broadleaf. **Other**: hedgerows by
  style, shapes by their label (shade casters said to cast shade), measurements.
  **Analysis**: the sun path, shade, contours and wind, each only while drawn.
- **The switches are remembered** between sessions (QSettings, `src/legend_flow.py`,
  the V3.06 north-arrow pattern), sent to the page on every load.
- **Print**: Export PDF's first page is a grab of the map as shown, legend
  included, so the switches print as two small words; the naming pencil appears
  only while the pointer is over the legend, so it does not.

### Found while testing it

- **A fence could not be drawn.** The legend probe drew a fence through
  `loadHedgerow` and the page threw: `finishHedgerow` measured a line's length
  only for the styles that dot plants along it, and a fence dots none, so its
  tooltip line hit `undefined.toFixed`. Everything after that line never ran:
  the fence stayed on the map but was never kept, never sent to Python, never
  saved, and the tool stayed in drawing mode. Draw › Shape has offered Fence
  since V3.07. The length is measured for every style now, and a line without
  plants does not claim "~0 plants".
- **The numbers stood over plants too small to hold them.** The legend's zoom
  handler was hooked before any plant existed, so on a zoom it ran before the
  plants worked out their new size, and read the old one. It works the
  on-screen radius out from the plant's metres at the current zoom instead.
- **The boundary menu's colour swatches sat under Remove Boundary**, away from
  their "Color:" label: the menu adds the swatch row after its last item.
  "Color:" is the last item now.

## Left alone, on purpose

- **No names on the map itself.** The owner asked to tell boundaries apart in the
  legend; the colour does it on the map. A name in the area label is a small
  later step if wanted.
- **The relationship web, the bee view and the slope and water overlays** keep
  their own legends; the map's legend does not repeat them.
- **Notes and the site pin** are labelled where they are drawn and were never in
  the legend.
- **A Shape line's colour, width and spacing are not saved** (found reading
  `_on_hedgerow_complete`: it writes green, 1.5 m and 1 m whatever was drawn, so
  a brown fence reopens green). Older than this and not asked about; suggested
  as its own task.
- **Undo's status word for a plants-only delete** reads "delete selection" now
  where it read "remove plants", since every Delete goes through one handler.

## What could not be verified here

- **The packaged app on Windows**, and the boundary name dialog as drawn by Qt:
  the probes drive the real page in Chromium, which is what QtWebEngine is, but
  not inside Qt's web view, and the dialog was tested with its answer patched.
- **How the legend looks in Export PDF**: the first page is a grab of the map
  as shown, and no display here can take one. The switches will print as two
  small words; the naming pencil should not, as it shows only under the pointer.
