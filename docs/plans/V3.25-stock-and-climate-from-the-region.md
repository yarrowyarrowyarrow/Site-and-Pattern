## Availability from the nurseries' stock

**What could be measured here: nothing.** The container's network policy
refuses every nursery's site (`CONNECT 403`), and the web tools could not
resolve them, nor the City of Edmonton's *Native Plants Suppliers
Availability List* (a PDF a search found, which would have been the best
source: it is the City's own table of species by supplier). So the stock has to
come from the owner, and this increment builds the way in rather than inventing
data. The V3.14 tiers were already my guess, and the owner has rejected a
guess.

**The levels** (`src/local_stock.py`), for the region's own natives only:

| Level | When |
|---|---|
| Common | listed as plants by at least two of the region's nurseries, on at least half of each one's lists; or the owner says so |
| Sometimes available | listed by one nursery, or only on some of their lists (the owner's harebell and prairie sage) |
| Seed only | listed only as seed |
| Hard to find | on no list once three nurseries' lists are on file, or a restoration plant (below) |
| Not checked yet | no list and no answer covers it |

- **A restoration plant is never Common**, whatever a list or an answer says:
  the 23 Edmonton natives the catalogue already ranks `rare`, each with its
  reason in `sourcing_notes` (orchids, hemiparasites, bog plants, mountain or
  far-north ranges, few records). That is the owner's rule.
- **Not checked is not hard to find** (P9). Two lists missing a plant say
  little; three say it is hard to find.
- **Only stock makes a plant easiest.** A plant no list covers keeps its old
  tier for the generator's ranking, capped at the second rung
  (`sourcing.ease`), so nothing reaches the top on my V3.14 judgment alone.
  *Easy to find* and the budget goal keep Common and Sometimes, and where the
  stock is silent they keep what is not known to be seed-only or rare, as before.
- **A Calgary nursery's list counts only for Edmonton's natives.** Anything
  else on it is kept in the file and never ranked.
- The owner's six named plants are the first answers: Boreal Yarrow (the
  catalogue's *Achillea borealis*, which nurseries sell as *A. millefolium*),
  Prairie Crocus, Wild Bergamot and Giant Hyssop Common; Harebell and Prairie
  Sage Sometimes. Today: 4 common, 2 sometimes, 23 hard to find, 221 not checked.

**Where it shows**: the picker's filter (*Availability*, five levels, in place
of *Where to buy*), *Easy to find*, the budget goal, the generator's ranking,
the plant page ("Common at Edmonton native-plant nurseries (Arnica
Wildflowers and …)", "Sold now and then …: check before you go"), and the order
file's *Where to try* column, which names the nurseries whose lists carry the
plant. The website keeps `availability_class` (no website work for now, G5).

**The way in**: the stock review page
(<https://claude.ai/artifact/M34LyMq1RPqXcNRTLuDmwW>, `tools/stock_review/`).
Paste a nursery's published list and it finds the plants by common name,
binomial, the catalogue's renames and a small table of the older names
nurseries use (`src/stock_lists.py`), showing every line it could not read; or
mark plants Common, Sometimes, Seed only or Hard to find. It previews each
plant's level with the same rules. `scripts/merge_stock.py --merge` folds the
page's rows in, reading each list again with the Python matcher and checking
every nursery and name; `--list FILE` takes a saved text file directly. No
schema bump: the stock is read from file, like the Edmonton list.

## Climate and coverage from the region (ED-6 to ED-8)

- **One frost pair.** The sun path said May 7 / Sep 23, the pack May 15 /
  Sep 16, the calendar's notes ~May 20. `src/region_climate.py` reads the
  pack's station normals; the sun path's presets, Site Info's frost row (when
  the site's own window is missing) and the plan's default planting window use
  it. Decision E7: City Centre's dates, as the pack already had.
- **The Water page's fallback rainfall is the pack's normals**, named with the
  station and period, where it was a hand-rounded copy labelled "Edmonton
  normal". Growing-season rain from the pack: 340.5 mm May–Sep.
- **Fallbacks stop at 150 km.** Rainfall's last resort was the nearest bundled
  normal at any distance, and soil and wind had no limit at all, so an offline
  Toronto pin was given Regina's rain (R32). Now none of the three stands in
  past 150 km (`property_data.FALLBACK_MAX_KM`).
- **Coverage messages (§4.5)** on Site Info, under the pin, and at the top of
  Plan My Yard. Outside Alberta and Saskatchewan, Plan My Yard's button is off
  with *"Plan My Yard needs a pin in Alberta or Saskatchewan."* (decision E3);
  Site Info and the status bar never say "Site data ready" there. A pin in the
  provinces outside the pack plans with the province's natives and the pack's
  dates, with the warning (E2).
- **The map** opens on the pack's view when a design has nothing to show; the
  address box's words and the contour download's name come from the pack, and
  the download shows only when the pack names a city's contours. The example
  opens at the pack's centre for a pin outside every pack.

## Native for the design's pin (ED-9, ED-17)

`src/design_native.py` is one rule: the region pack's list inside a pack or
with no pin (E5), the pin's province elsewhere (ED-9), Alberta outside both.
The habitat score's native term and its nudge ("aren't native around
Edmonton"), the Habitat page, the Report card, the Water page, the plan, the
PDF, the care hours, community facts and the swap ranking ask it; the design
sync stamps each placed plant once.

**A deliberate headline change**, measured with the old and new rule on the
same designs:

| Design | Score | Natives |

| The worked example (Edmonton) | 64 → 64 | 14/14 → 14/14 |
| Generated, Edmonton, pollinators | 90 → 87 | 27/27 → 23/27 |

The Edmonton designs above were planned without the *Edmonton natives only*
goal; with it (the first-run default) every plant is on the list. The design
critic compares two scores of the same design by the same rule, so its
adopt-if-better test is unaffected.

## V3.24's red Tests run, found and fixed here

V3.24's installers built, but its Tests run failed on the region guard
(`site_panel.py` 7 and `controllers/map_events.py` 2 against its ratchet). The
guard tokenizes the source, and CI runs Python 3.11, where an f-string is one
string token; sessions run 3.13, where an f-string is split into pieces the
guard skipped, so it passed here and failed there. It now rebuilds each
f-string as one token on every version (a test says so), and the two contour
download messages it then found name the pack.

## Decided, and why

- **The stock lives in the pack**, beside the local list, because it is true
  of one place: a Calgary pack would carry its own nurseries' lists.

  that most plants are not checked yet.
