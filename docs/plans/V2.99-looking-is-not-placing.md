# V2.99 — Looking is not placing

*One item: **F191**, step 1 of the V2.98 review of perusing, selecting and placing
plants and communities: "Separate looking from placing. Selecting shows details.
Placing starts from an explicit Place action, the map shows a clear armed state
(banner, crosshair, footprint under the cursor), and Esc or Cancel syncs
everywhere. Clears 8, 9, part of 10, and 18's list-arming problem." The owner chose
it next: "Next do 1. Separate looking from placing."*

**This reverses a decision made from feedback, on the owner's call.** Selecting has
armed the map since V2.37, when a tester wrote: "selecting a plant or plant
community should be sufficient to then place that unit on the map... often I end up
placing the wrong thing (the last thing) because I haven't hit the button." The
backlog row for F191 said so and left it to the owner, who has now decided. The
design below keeps what that tester needed where it matters: once you are placing,
choosing another plant is what you place next. What changes is that choosing, while
nothing is being placed, is only looking.

## What was wrong

Measured on V2.98 (dc4e4e2) by driving the real panels offscreen with simulated
mouse and keyboard input (`QTest`) and recording every arming announcement:

| Gesture | Arms the map? |
|---|---|
| Tab keyboard focus into the plant list, nothing pressed | **yes**, with the first plant (Arum-leaved Arrowhead, a pond plant) |
| Click a plant's name | **yes** |
| Click ▶ to read the plant's card | **yes** |
| Down arrow through the list | **yes**, each step |
| Right-click → Add to current mix, map idle | no (fixed in V2.98) |
| Tab focus into the community list | **yes**, with the first community |
| Click a community to read about it | **yes** |

So every way of *looking* at a plant or a community, including arriving at the list
by keyboard, readies the next click on the map to plant it. The review found the
rest (finding 8): after placing, the mode stays on, so every further click plants
another; the only sign was status-bar text. V2.98 put a bar over the map that says
"Placing Wild Bergamot" while it is armed, which answered the *invisible* half. The
other half is that looking should not ready a click at all.

Three more things came out of reading the code for this:

- **A pattern silently swaps the plant for the mix.** `_current_pattern()` attaches
  the mix's recipe to every Row, Grid or Circle whenever a mix of two or more species
  exists, so with a mix built, picking Wild Bergamot and choosing Row places a row of
  the *mix*. What was asked for and what the map plants came apart again, one level
  down.
- **After a search the panel keeps a selection nobody can see.** A search resets the
  list model, which drops the highlighted row, but `_selected_plant` keeps the old
  plant. With selection as the arming gesture that was harmless; a Place button
  naming a plant that is not on screen would not be.
- **`activated` is not "Enter or double-click" everywhere.** Both panels arm on
  `activated` / `itemActivated`, which Qt emits on a *single* click wherever the
  style says items activate on one click (KDE's default, among others). Arming there
  would survive this change unnoticed on the machines it was tested on.

## The change

### Selecting is looking

A click, an arrow key, or keyboard focus arriving in either list selects; nothing
else. The plant list highlights the row and names it on the Place button; the
community list shows its detail pane, as it always has. **Nothing reaches the map
while it is idle.**

### Placing starts from a Place action

Each of these arms the map, and only these:

| | Plants | Communities |
|---|---|---|
| A Place button that names what it places | "Place Wild Bergamot on the map", under the list | "Place on map" beside the community's name; its accessible name says which community |
| Double-click | the row | the community |
| Enter or Return | on the selected row | on the selected community |
| Right-click | "Place Wild Bergamot on Map", "Place ×5 on Map" (kept) | — |
| The mix | Place mix (kept) | Place mix (kept) |

Enter is handled as a key on the list, not through `activated`, for the single-click
platforms above. The buttons are actions, not toggles: pressing Place while already
placing that plant re-arms it with the bar's settings. The V2.37 button toggled, so
after Esc it took two clicks to arm again (finding 9).

### While placing, the list is a palette

Once the map is placing, a completed click on another plant, or an arrow key onto
it, switches the placement to it: the bar's sentence changes, and the next click on
the map plants what you just chose. This is the V2.37 tester's case, kept: you
cannot place "the last thing" once you have chosen another. Two limits make it
predictable:

- **A drag is not a choice.** Plants are dragged into the mix. A press that turns into
  a drag selects the row but does not switch the placement; only a click that ends on
  the row it started on does. (Keyboard moves switch at once; there is no drag.)
- **The other tab's list is only looking.** Placing a plant, clicking a community
  shows the community; it does not take the map. Each panel switches only what it
  armed. Its own Place button still takes over, and the other panel stands down, as
  in V2.98.

### What you placed is what is placed

The subject of a placement is what the Place action named: a plant, or the mix. The
pattern says only how. A plant in Row is a row of that plant, mix or no mix; only
Place mix places the mix. Two rules from V2.98 stay, because a mix cannot go down one
plant per click: Place mix from Single switches the bar to Row, and choosing Single
while placing a mix sets the mix aside for the selected plant, or stands the map down
if none is selected. The bar says which, as before.

### The map shows it is armed

- **Banner**: the V2.98 bar ("Placing Wild Bergamot. Click the map to place it."),
  which now adds "Each click places another." for Single plants and communities,
  because staying armed after a click was the other half of finding 8.
- **Crosshair**: `setMode` already sets it for all three placing modes, and its
  `placing` class keeps it over interactive layers; checked in the running app,
  since Leaflet puts its own grab cursor on the map container.
- **A footprint under the cursor**, in the pattern preview's language (a yellow
  ring at the planting spacing, a green one at mature canopy), at the radius a placed
  marker is drawn (`placePlantMarker`: half the spacing, floored at 5 cm), so the
  ghost is the size of what lands: one plant in Single;
  a Qty cluster exactly as `_hexBurstPositions` will lay it, with its count; the
  start plant of a Row, Grid or Circle before its first click (the existing preview
  takes over after it); and a single community's members at their offsets inside a
  ring at its extent, computed from the same `offset_x`/`offset_y` arithmetic
  `_on_polyculture_click` uses, so the ghost is where the plants land. Fill Area
  keeps its polygon preview, which is its footprint.

The footprint lives in a new `html/map/08-footprint.js`: `03-plants.js`, where the
pattern preview is, is at 950 of its 950 lines.

### Esc and Done

V2.98 made the map report every mode it enters, so Esc in the map, Done in the bar,
and Esc with focus in a panel (MainWindow's `keyPressEvent`) all stand both panels
down. Nothing changes there; a test now covers Esc pressed while the Place button has
focus, since pressing it leaves the keyboard in the panel rather than on the map.

## Found on the way

- **Qt 6 reports ▶ as a click.** The plant row's delegate takes the release on its
  chevron, and Qt 5 then emitted no `clicked`; Qt 6 emits it anyway, before checking
  whether the delegate took it. So reading a card while placing switched the
  placement to it, in the first draft. The delegate now says so (`took_click()`),
  and `ListGestures` asks.
- **The pattern preview drew rings too big for small plants.** Its planting-spacing
  ring had a 0.5 m floor, where a placed marker's is 5 cm (a comment in
  `placePlantMarker` records why the old 1 m floor went). A forb spaced 0.3 m
  previewed as a 1 m disc and landed as a 0.3 m one. The first draft of the footprint
  copied it; both now use the marker's floor. `03-plants.js` stays at 950 of 950.
- **A built mix rode along on any plant's Row, Grid or Circle** (above).
- **After a search, the panel kept a selection nobody could see** (above).

## Left alone, on purpose

- **What "looking" shows for a plant.** The painted card behind ▶ stays until step 2
  (F192) puts the Directory's species page there. Step 1 separates the two acts; step
  2 improves what looking shows.
- **Warnings after placing** onto other plants or across the boundary (the rest of
  finding 10). A footprint shows the size before the click; what to say about a
  collision is its own question, numbered **F198**, since none of the review's six
  steps answered it.
- **A community's Row, Grid and Circle ghost** still draws its preview member's canopy
  at community spacing, as V2.98 left it; the whole community per anchor is step 6's
  (F196).
- **Putting a plant down without a pointer** (finding 18's last bullet). Every Place
  action now works from the keyboard, but the map still needs a click; that belongs
  with the accessibility baseline (F195).

## What could not be verified here

- **KDE, Windows and macOS.** The single-click-activation case was tested with a
  stand-in style, not on a KDE desktop; drags were real X drags in Xvfb, not Windows
  or macOS drag and drop. Qt emits no `clicked` after a drag on any of them, but that
  is Qt's documented behaviour, not something run here.
- **Screen readers, heard.** The Place buttons' names are asserted from Qt's
  accessible names; the AT-SPI tree was not re-dumped this time, and nothing was
  listened to.
- **On aerial imagery.** The container has no tiles, so the footprint was seen on the
  plain background only. Its discs are faint (15% fill) by design, to show position
  without hiding what is under them; on imagery they may need more.
- **`tests/test_undo_redo.py`**, which segfaults in a container (CLAUDE.md) and which
  CI also leaves out; a desktop run is still owed.
