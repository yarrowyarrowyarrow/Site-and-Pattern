# V3.05 — Every surface, counted

*The owner's overnight ask, after a list of what could be worked on unattended:
"Bundle 1 2 and 3 into 1 version release. I want a particular focus on 3 so
perhaps start there." Group 3 was the retirement pass F94 (a task-shaped home)
and F89 (the 3D preview's review) both say must come before any restructure,
**what is genuinely load-bearing?**; group 1 the leftovers of the V2.98 review of
picking and placing (F196, F198, F199, F200); group 2 the rest of the
take-it-outside document (F123, F32, F19). The decisions waiting on the owner
(F170, F184, F203, F201, F169) were not answered and were not touched, nor were
F189 (a P12 question) and the bee photograph licence.*

## What was measured

From the real window, not from the source. `scripts/surface_inventory.py` boots
`MainWindow` in a throwaway home folder with the worked example open (19 plants,
a boundary, a pin) at 1366 × 768 in Liberation Sans (Arial's metrics), walks every
tab at every depth, records each control (kind, words, tooltip, screen-reader
name, whether anything listens to it, how far down its column it sits), clicks
every enabled button with every dialog, question, file picker and web link
intercepted and cancelled, and opens every window and dialog it can reach. It
writes `inventory.json` and a screenshot per page.

The audit it fed is [`docs/SURFACE_AUDIT.md`](../SURFACE_AUDIT.md): 6 tabs, 26
pages, 169 controls, 27 menu items, 19 toolbar items, 5 windows, 7 dialogs, and
1,816 words on the pages, 1,252 of them in paragraphs of 15 words or more on 20 of
the 26. Its findings are grouped by kind (results that wait for a button, one
thing in several places, one word for two things, instructions where the page
should speak, pointers that point nowhere, sizes that only fit a bigger screen,
asking for what the design knows, empty states that look broken), then the tasks
walked click by click, then **a keep / merge / move / retire proposal for every
page with an empty Decision column, which is the owner's**, and a sketch of four
tabs (Site, Plants, Design, Learn) and one Share place in which every page has a
place. A page made from it is published for reading on a phone, and it records
the owner's choice for each page and question
(<https://claude.ai/artifact/Q57cbnchvARZBSgiiBojqE>, private to the owner; a
session reads them with `ArtifactData` `list` on `decisions`). Only the owner and
editors can write there; a Contributor-level write was checked and refused.

### The click pass

**One button killed the app.** Site › Slope › *Download Edmonton Data* raised
`ImportError: cannot import name '_USER_AGENT' from 'src.terrain'` on every
click: the constant had moved to `src/http_utils.py` and the downloader still
imported it from where it had been. `tests/test_imports_resolved.py` exists for
exactly this, and passed, because it checked that the name was *bound* in the
importing module, which an import statement does even when its source no longer
has the name. And nothing caught the exception: PyQt6 calls `qFatal` on an
exception that escapes a slot unless `sys.excepthook` is replaced, so the process
ended, unsaved work and all, with the traceback on a stderr a packaged build does
not show and the app's log never receives.

**No dead controls.** 28 of 106 clicks changed nothing visible, and each is
explained in the audit: ticks that save after a pause, options for an overlay not
yet drawn, and toolbar entries that are labels.

**The map is not clicked by the probe**, and by reading the code: a click on a
placed plant sent `plant_marker_clicked` to Python, and **nothing in Python
listened**. The click only changed what a drag moves.

## The change

### What the audit found broken

- **The crash.** The downloader imports `_USER_AGENT` from `src.http_utils`. The
  import guard gained `TestFromImportsResolve`: every `from src.x import name`
  must find `name` defined in `src/x.py`. Against the old tree it fails on this
  line and on nothing else.
- **An exception in a slot is survived** (`src/slot_errors.py`, installed from
  `main.py` only): logged with its traceback, reported in a non-blocking message
  that says where the log is and to save, once per distinct fault and at most
  three a minute, and the event loop goes on. Carrying on can leave one panel
  half-updated; every desktop app that does this makes that trade, and here the
  alternative was losing the session. A subprocess test proves the premise (the
  process aborts without the hook) and the cure (it survives with it).
- **Five results that waited for a button follow the design**
  (`src/live_refresh.py`): Analysis › Habitat, Planning › Effort, Wildlife,
  Harvest and Water. Each panel was already handed the plants and structures on
  every edit, so the button only gated work the app could do, and its result went
  stale with the next edit while saying nothing. The page on screen is refilled
  when it is shown and 150 ms after edits (a community of eight is one refill);
  a page nobody looks at is not computed. The buttons are gone.
  `analysis_panel.set_placed_plants` had named this class in V2.42 ("the stale-list
  bug") and moved three read-outs off their buttons one release at a time; the
  score they all explain was the one left.
- **Planning › Water starts from the design** (`src/design_inputs.py`): the
  boundary's area and the barrels, ponds and swales placed. It had started from
  200 m² and 2 barrels whatever the yard; the example's is 88 m².
- **Generate Design fits a 768 px screen.** It was 769 px tall, so on the most
  common laptop its Generate button was off the bottom. The goals and options
  scroll inside a dialog capped to the screen; the buttons stay put. Its animal
  list gained a find box.
- **The 3D preview's sliders have room.** Year, Time of year and Time of day were
  15 px wide at the 1148 px the window opens at on that screen, a handle that
  looked like a checkbox, because the same row held the detail level and five
  buttons. The row is *when*, and the buttons moved to the third row.
- **"Sun & Sh…" and "Timel…" fit their tabs**: each tab is given its natural
  width and an equal share of the slack.
- **Pointers that pointed nowhere.** The worked example's notes sent a new user to
  "Planning → Planting Plan" (it is File → Export Planting Plan…) and the score's
  tips to "Plants → Use → Keystone" two releases after Use became Role. Both
  fixed, and `test_app_smoke` now reads every "A → B" written in the source whose
  A is a side tab, a menu or a toolbar row and fails when B is not in the real
  window: 40 such pointers, these two stale.
- **Smaller**: Planning › Notes said "0 words" over a page of notes until the
  first keystroke; Growth Snapshots promised years 1, 5, 15 and 30 above panels
  for 1, 5, 15 and 20 and drew an 11 × 8 m yard in a 50 m frame; Wind asked for a
  pin that was down; Bees printed a nameless bee's binomial twice and a grey "—"
  pill beside every plant of unknown fit; the WebGL failure read "Uncaught Error:
  Error creating WebGL context."; Stats and Present gave two native shares of one
  design without saying what each counted; Structures › Habitat drew three empty
  boxes (a border meant for the frame reached the labels inside it); a new design
  was named "My Food Forest". All fixed.

### Group 2: the take-it-outside document

- **F123.** The 3D window keeps the last still it renders on the main window and
  Export PDF passes it, so F69's page reaches the PDF. A new or opened design

- **F32.** Site › Field Notes › *Print this sheet…*: the ten questions on one page,

- **F19.** Every generated plant carries why it is where it is (`why_here` on its

  placed by rules. Each rule now names itself (`src/why_here.py`); the same probe
  reads 108 of 108.

### Found on the way

- **The F19 click hook would have stopped the window being built.** It connected
  to `map_widget.plant_marker_clicked`, which lives on `map_widget.bridge`. The
  smoke test written for it caught it before anything was pushed: every other test
  in its class skipped with "MainWindow construction failed". CI would have failed
  on the skip count.
- **The picker passed its line ceiling** (578 against 560) with F200, and its
  quality toggles went to `filter_status.QualityChips`, as its chips did in V3.01.
- **The five F199 rows were in no community.** Counted as natives now, they tripped
  the guard that every retail native has one. Each went into the community its
  catalogue notes describe: False Box under Montane Woodland Beauty's fir, the
  aster in Wet Meadow Aster & Monkeyflower, the alumroot on Montane Scree, the
  sunflower in Mixedgrass Late Aster & Goldenrod, the anemone at Parkland Woodland
  Wildflowers' edge, each at least 1.5 m from every other member, the communities'
  own spacing. Within schema 92, which re-seeds communities.
- **F167 was done two releases ago.** Black Hawthorn arrived with `has_thorns` when
  F161 promoted it in V2.87; the backlog and the ledger kept it open.
- **A correction to the audit's first draft**: the "empty dark square" on the Bees
  page is the 🐝 shown when there is no photograph, drawn in a font with no emoji.
  Only the probe's machine lacks one. Not changed.
- **Do not edit source while the suite runs.** One test reads a function's source
  with `inspect.getsource`, which uses the loaded code's line numbers against the
  file on disk; an edit mid-run shifted the lines and it read the wrong function.
  It passes alone and in the second run.

## What could not be verified here

- **A real desktop.** Everything ran under a virtual display on Linux: Windows and
  macOS draw their own controls and fonts, and CI's DejaVu Sans is about 12% wider
  than the Arial metrics measured here.
- **A printer.** The site-walk sheet was checked as a PDF and as a rendered image,
  not on paper.
- **The network.** Wind, soil and elevation could not be fetched, so the "Why here"
  words seen end to end were the no-site-data ones; the scored words are covered by
  tests against made-up cells.
- **The marker click itself.** The test emits the bridge signal the map's click
  handler sends; the handler is unchanged since before V3.00.
- **What anyone actually uses.** The desktop app sends nothing, on purpose; the
  audit says *works, reachable, not said better elsewhere*, not *used*.
- **`tests/test_undo_redo.py`**, which segfaults in its own teardown wherever there
  is no GPU (see CLAUDE.md). The landing note's Undo is the window's own.
