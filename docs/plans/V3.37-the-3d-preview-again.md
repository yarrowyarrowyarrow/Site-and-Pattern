# V3.37 · The 3D preview, working again

*The owner sent a screenshot of View › 3D Preview in fly mode (Half-black Bumble
Bee, July, 13:00, Lifelike): a dark scene, the bee in front of a black mass, and
the banner "3D viewer error: Uncaught ReferenceError: buildStructures is not
defined". "The 3D preview regressed and needs work."*

## What was wrong

**V3.22 deleted `buildStructures`.** RM-2 (`48a3c84`, "Import Yard Scan and the
splat backdrop leave the app") removed 127 lines from
`html/scene3d/05-flowers.js`: `buildScanPoints`, then `buildStructures`, then the
splat section. `buildStructures` was the yard's own structures (bee hotel, brush
pile, pond, rain barrel), not the scan's, and sat between the two scan functions.
`permaSetScene` still called it, so from V3.22 to here every scene push threw
right after `buildPlants`. Nothing after that line ran:

- `setDay` / `setSun` (the sun for the chosen month and hour) and `fitShadow`;
- `frameCamera`, so the orbit was never framed on the design;
- `rebuildBeacons` on a re-push, `buildWalkObstacles`, `buildCritterObstacles`;
- the HUD line, which is where the error banner sat instead.

Reproduced in headless Chromium on the worked example: `PAGEERROR
buildStructures is not defined` on every push, and the scene left half built.

## Why nothing caught it for fifteen releases

Three gates boot the real viewer in headless Chromium and push a scene:
`test_scene3d_render` (aspect), `test_slope_render` (which has a test called
*a structure box stands on the ground*) and `test_accuracy_render`. Each would
have failed. None ran:

1. **CI read the throw as "no WebGL".** A probe writes its result into the page
   title when it finishes; a viewer that throws never finishes, and all three
   tests read an unwritten title as *"the probe never reported — no WebGL in
   this environment"* and skipped. The V3.37 CI run lists exactly that, 3 times,
   among its 21 skips. CI has Chrome and WebGL (SwiftShader); since every run
   from V3.22 on was green, those three can only have skipped on every one of
   them.
2. **In a session they never started.** `_find_chromium` looked for Playwright's
   `chrome-linux/chrome`; the container's Chromium is `chrome-linux64/chrome`.
   Every browser gate in the suite skipped with "no Chromium binary".
3. **Nothing reads the chunks for names.** The viewer and the map are classic
   scripts sharing one global scope; a call to a function no chunk declares is
   found only when the line runs.

## What changed

- **`buildStructures` is back** in `05-flowers.js`, as it was (the Blender GLB
  first, a box as the fallback), with a line saying why it is there.
- **A thrown probe fails** (`html/probe_watch.js`, loaded by the five 3D probe
  pages). It writes every error the probe page catches, and every one the viewer
  reports through `_showViewerError`, into `<pre id="probe-errors">`, which
  `--dump-dom` carries out whatever the title says;
  `tests/test_scene3d_render.py:fail_on_viewer_errors` fails on it before
  looking for the result. A viewer that could not start for want of WebGL sets
  `permaBootError` and is not recorded, so a machine without WebGL still skips.
  The viewer's missing `qrc:` script outside Qt is not recorded either. Checked
  both ways: with `buildStructures` taken out again, all three gates fail with
  *"probe: Uncaught ReferenceError: buildStructures is not defined"*; with it
  in, all pass.
- **`_find_chromium` knows `chrome-linux64`.** Every browser test now runs in a
  session.
- **`tests/test_js_names.py`, no browser, never skipped:** reads every chunk of
  the viewer and of the map with comments, strings and regular expressions
  blanked, collects every declared name (functions, variables, parameters,
  `window.x =`, the page's own scripts) and fails on a bare call to anything
  else that is not a browser built-in. Both trees are clean today; with the
  deletion put back it reports `{'buildStructures': ['05-flowers.js']}`.
- `probe_watch.js` is a developer file: the installer leaves it out with the
  probe pages (`_NOT_SHIPPED`, and its test).

## The dark view: explained in part

The banner is explained completely. The **darkness is not**: headless runs of
the broken viewer on the worked example, flat, on a 12 m slope and on a yard 45
m above a valley, stayed in daylight with the bee among the flowers. What they
did not have is the owner's own design, Qt WebEngine and a real GPU on Windows.

Chasing it found a second fault, older than V3.22: **Fly as a bee starts
wherever the design centre +6 m south falls**, 1.6 m up, inside whatever stands
there. With a shrub on that spot the view is its stems as two dark walls either
side of the bee. The walker had the same fault and was moved off trunks in
V2.46; the bee never was. `clearOfCrowns` (`08-modes.js`, beside the walker's
obstacles, because `06-fly.js` is at 941 of its 950 lines) steps the start south
until it clears every crown taller than 1.2 m and every building;
`tests/test_bee_spawn.py` runs it in node. Whether this was the owner's
darkness is a guess until they look again.

The screenshot's other line, *"nothing it drinks from is in bloom in July"*,
did not reproduce either: on the worked example, Half-black Bumble Bee has four
plants in flower in July (Blanketflower, Boreal Yarrow, Purple Prairie Clover,
Wild Bergamot) and the fixed viewer shows beacons over them. It may be true of
the owner's design.

## Left alone

- **The orbit frames the ±25 m stage, not the yard.** `build_scene` gives every
  scene a floor of ±25 m and `frameCamera` frames that, so the worked example's
  11 × 8 m yard is small in the middle of the first view. It has been so since
  long before V3.22; framing on the boundary and plants is a small change, and
  the owner's to want.
- **CI's `map.html … loadPlantMarker is not defined` lines.** Every function they
  name exists (`05-features.js`); they are calls made into a map page that has
  not finished loading, at test teardown. Not this fault.
- The probe pages that drive the 2D map (boundary, legend, measure, offline,
  select-all) do not load the watcher; their tests already fail rather than skip
  when the page does not report.
