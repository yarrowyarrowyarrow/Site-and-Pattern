Wildlife/Black-tailed Bumble Bee

Black-tailed Bumble Bee

Bombus melanopygus native bee

A medium-tongued social bumble bee. Nests underground and above ground, sometimes in birdhouses or building insulation. Forages on Arctostaphylos, Eriogonum, Haplopappus, Lupinus and others.
