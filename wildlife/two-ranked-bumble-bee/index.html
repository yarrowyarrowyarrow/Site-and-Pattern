Wildlife/Two-ranked Bumble Bee

Two-ranked Bumble Bee

Bombus bifarius native bee

A medium-tongued social bumble bee. Nests usually underground, sometimes on the surface. Forages on Aster, Epilobium, Haplopappus, Lupinus and others.
